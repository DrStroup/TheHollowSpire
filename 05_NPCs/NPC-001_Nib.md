# NPC-001 — Nib

Status: LOCKED v1.0  
Completion: Development Complete + Visualized

## Classification

NPC  
Role: Survivor, Potential Companion, Floor 1 Ally

## Overview

Nib is the first friendly face most explorers encounter within the Hollow Spire. He is alive, adaptable, and hopeful.

## Appearance

Small stature, thin frame, patchwork clothing, improvised tools, quick movements.

Distinguishing features:
- Lantern charm
- Scrap knife
- Mismatched boots
- Too-large backpack

## Personality

Helpful, observant, brave when it matters. Also impulsive, talkative, and overconfident.

## Goals

Immediate: Stay alive.  
Personal: Understand the Spire.  
Hidden: Find proof that escape is possible.

## Fears

Admitted: Broken Custodians, dark tunnels, being alone.  
Unadmitted: Being forgotten.

## Secrets

- Has limited local experience and survival knowledge; does not provide an authoritative route map.
- Has seen the Silent Jailer.
- Has followed the Lost Child and does not discuss the outcome.

## Companion Rule

Nib should never overshadow players. He shares practical survival observations and may accompany the party, but is not an established guide or a primary combatant. He does not solve navigation or mysteries for the party.

## Introduction

First seen moving through the Rotunda from the Abandoned Guard Post in Zone 01. First meaningful conversation: Zone 02, the Rotunda. The collapsed-maintenance-niche rescue is superseded by user decision CAN-001 (2026-09-16).
