Keyed Areas
A. Cell Block A - Starting cells, graffiti, prisoner encounters.
B. Cell Block B - Repeating/missing cell numbers.
C. Rotunda - Party reunion, records office, explorer satchel clue.
D. Shattered Corridor - Nib trapped under rubble.
E. Maintenance Hall - Ancient machinery and lantern symbols.
F. Hidden Observation Chamber - Secret records, Moon Lantern access.
G. Cell 11 - Empty cell that should not exist.
H. Breach Zone - Flooding escape route to Floor 2.

Secrets
- Observation chamber inaccessible initially.
- Cell 11 exists despite records denying it.
- Lantern symbols predate the prison.
