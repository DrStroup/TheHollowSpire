# 0.7 Leave the World Better Than You Found It

The Hollow Spire is filled with mysteries waiting to be uncovered, dangers waiting to be overcome, and wonders waiting to be discovered.

Yet exploration alone is not the purpose of the Explorer Guild.

The Guild exists because people rely upon one another.

Every settlement depends upon routes that must be maintained. Every traveler depends upon those who came before. Every expedition builds upon knowledge earned through the efforts and sacrifices of countless others.

For this reason, explorers are encouraged to follow a simple principle:

Leave the world better than you found it.

Sometimes this means repairing a damaged bridge.

Sometimes it means documenting a safer route through dangerous territory.

Sometimes it means helping a struggling settlement, sharing valuable knowledge, or ensuring that a hard-learned lesson is not forgotten.

Not every improvement must be grand.

Small actions often have lasting consequences.

A lantern relit along an abandoned road may guide travelers for years. A warning marker placed beside a hidden hazard may prevent future tragedies. A single act of kindness may create opportunities that ripple outward long after an expedition has ended.

The greatest explorers are rarely remembered solely for the places they reached.

They are remembered for the changes they made possible.

Throughout this book, you will encounter opportunities to restore outposts, reconnect communities, preserve knowledge, improve routes, assist those in need, and contribute to the future of the Spire.

These opportunities are not distractions from the journey.

They are part of the journey.

Every expedition leaves a mark upon the world.

The question every explorer must answer is simple:

What kind of mark will you leave behind?
