# Book 01 Scope Decisions v1.0

## Status

Locked for current production.

## Scope

Book 01 covers Region 001: The Blackwater Warrens.

Included floors:

1. The Black Cells
2. Flooded Paths
3. Emberlight Village
4. The Drowned Road
5. Shadow Marsh
6. Blackwater Run
7. Lantern Village
8. The Veiled Warrens
9. Whispering Deep
10. Moon Lantern Sanctuary
11. Truths Below
12. The First Seal

## Finale

Book 01 culminates in the First Seal of Truth. The Moon Lantern is recovered before the finale and opens hidden routes, contracts, chambers, and Book 2 paths.

## Superseded Scope

Any older reference to Book 01 as Floors 1-10 is superseded by this decision.
