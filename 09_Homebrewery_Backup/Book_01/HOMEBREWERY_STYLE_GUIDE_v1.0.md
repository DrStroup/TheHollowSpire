# Homebrewery Style Guide v1.0

## Drafting Standards

- One major section per page break during drafting.
- Use boxed text only for table-ready read-aloud moments.
- Keep secrets in GM-facing sections, not player-facing handouts.
- Use consistent labels for floors, encounters, NPCs, maps, bestiary entries, discoveries, and contracts.
- Player-facing text should emphasize lived-in world, wonder, survival, community, and discovery.
- GM-facing text may include hidden-lore context, but should avoid flattening mystery into exposition.

## Book 01 Tone

Darkness and pressure should be balanced by awe, human warmth, and perseverance. Horror without wonder becomes exhausting.

## Recurrence Style Rule

Never explain recurrence directly in player-facing prose. Use names, echoes, contradictions, familiar handwriting, impossible records, and environmental recognition instead.
