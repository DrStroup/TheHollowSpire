# Heraldry & Faction Identity Framework v1.1

## Core Rule

A crest should only appear when the document has a reason to display one.

Not every artifact requires a crest.

## Valid Crest Use

If a crest or seal is used, it must be one of the canon faction crests for the book.

Examples:

- Explorer Guild crest for official Guild material
- Pilgrim crest for Pilgrim records, sermons, or religious notices
- Settlement seal for settlement-issued documents
- No crest for unknown, recovered, personal, ancient, or unattributed fragments

## Forbidden

Do not use:

- Generic compass seals
- Random fantasy emblems
- Unassigned crests
- Decorative symbols pretending to be faction identity
