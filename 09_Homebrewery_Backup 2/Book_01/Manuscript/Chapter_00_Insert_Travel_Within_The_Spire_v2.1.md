# Chapter 00 Insert — Travel Within the Spire v2.1

## Placement
Recommended placement: redesigned Chapter 0 Page 10, beside or immediately after **The Explorer Guild** section.

## Artifact Type
Guild Training Manual artifact / official Explorer Guild instruction sheet.

## Purpose
Introduce the hard setting rule that the Spire prevents flight, teleportation, planar escape, and fast travel. This should be understood before Chapter 1 begins.

## Artifact Text

### TRAVEL WITHIN THE SPIRE

New recruits often ask why the Guild spends so much effort maintaining roads, bridges, lantern posts, lifts, ferries, waystations, and maps.

The answer is simple.

There are no shortcuts.

The Spire resists flight. Wings grow heavy in the depths. Creatures that might soar beneath open skies find themselves confined to glides, hops, and brief desperate bursts before the dark pulls them down again.

Teleportation fares no better. Spells and devices meant to carry travelers instantly from one place to another fizzle beneath the Spire's weight. Planar paths collapse. Doorways to elsewhere fail to open. The Spire does not easily let go of those within it.

Roads must be walked.

Supplies must be carried.

Every safe mile exists because someone mapped it, marked it, repaired it, defended it, or died making certain others could pass.

Remember this well:

A repaired road is worth more than a thousand impossible shortcuts.
