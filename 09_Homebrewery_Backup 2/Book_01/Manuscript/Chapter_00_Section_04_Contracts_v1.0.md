# 0.4 Contracts

No explorer can be everywhere at once.

Throughout the Spire, settlements face challenges that require capable hands and willing hearts. A damaged bridge may isolate a village from its neighbors. Dangerous creatures may threaten a trade route. A missing expedition may need to be found before its trail grows cold.

To meet these needs, communities often issue contracts.

A contract is a formal request for assistance. Some are posted publicly by settlements, guild halls, or outposts. Others are carried by messengers or shared through word of mouth. The scale of these requests varies greatly. One contract may involve delivering supplies to a nearby settlement, while another may require an expedition into unexplored territory.

Not every contract involves danger.

Some ask explorers to map a route, document unusual wildlife, recover historical records, investigate rumors, or establish contact with isolated communities. Others call for courage in the face of genuine threats.

Completing a contract benefits more than the individuals involved.

A repaired road may improve travel for years. A recovered map may guide future expeditions. A dangerous creature driven away from a settlement may save countless lives. Even small successes contribute to the broader effort of making the Spire safer, better understood, and more connected.

Rewards vary according to the needs of those offering the contract.

Some communities provide useful resources or materials. Others offer equipment, training, lodging, access to new routes, introductions to important figures, or opportunities that would otherwise remain unavailable. Many explorers find that the trust and goodwill earned through reliable service become valuable rewards in their own right.

The best contracts do more than solve a problem.

They create new opportunities for discovery.

Every request for help is also an invitation to learn something new about the Spire and the people who call it home.
