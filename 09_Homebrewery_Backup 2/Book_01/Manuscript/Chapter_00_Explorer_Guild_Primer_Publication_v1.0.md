# Chapter 00 — The Explorer Guild Primer

## 0.1 Welcome to the Hollow Spire

The Hollow Spire is a campaign of exploration, discovery, and restoration set within a vast subterranean world known simply as the Spire.

Beneath the surface lie forgotten settlements, abandoned outposts, ancient ruins, and mysteries that have endured for generations. Dangerous creatures roam the depths. Old routes collapse. New paths emerge. Entire communities struggle to survive in places where light is precious and certainty is rare.

Yet the Hollow Spire is not merely a tale of survival.

It is a story about what explorers leave behind.

Throughout this journey, characters will uncover hidden places, establish new routes, restore lost connections, and help the people who call the Spire home. Every discovery has the potential to change the world. Every repaired lantern, reopened passage, or completed contract leaves the depths a little brighter than before.

The Spire rewards curiosity. Those who venture beyond the safety of familiar roads will uncover forgotten histories, unusual resources, strange creatures, and wonders that few living souls have ever witnessed.

Danger is ever-present. Some challenges can be overcome through courage and determination. Others require preparation, patience, or the wisdom to retreat and return another day. Explorers who survive the longest are rarely the strongest. They are the ones who learn, adapt, and respect the unknown.

This book presents the first region of the Hollow Spire and the settlements, routes, contracts, and discoveries that await within it. Whether your expedition seeks knowledge, adventure, or a chance to leave a lasting mark upon the world, your journey begins here.

The path ahead descends into darkness.

Carry your light well.

### Explorer's Note #1

A good explorer measures success by more than survival.

Did a route become safer?

Did a settlement receive help?

Did a mystery find an answer?

Did those who follow have an easier journey because you came before them?

If so, the expedition was worthwhile.

## 0.2 The Explorer Guild

No one knows exactly when the Explorer Guild was founded.

Some claim it began as a loose collection of scouts and cartographers who sought to map the safer passages between settlements. Others believe its origins lie in the earliest expeditions into the deeper reaches of the Spire, when survival depended upon cooperation between strangers.

Whatever its beginnings, the Guild has become one of the most respected institutions within the known depths.

Its members come from every walk of life. Couriers, laborers, scholars, hunters, guides, and adventurers alike may choose to take up the Guild's mantle. Some seek knowledge. Some seek purpose. Others simply wish to help those who depend upon the routes that connect the settlements of the Spire.

The Guild serves many roles.

Its members chart unexplored territory, record discoveries, investigate reports from the frontier, assist isolated communities, recover lost knowledge, and maintain the fragile network of routes that allow civilization to endure beneath the earth.

Many explorers spend their entire careers working close to home, helping maintain local roads and responding to nearby contracts. Others descend ever deeper, pursuing mysteries that few dare investigate.

The Guild does not measure success solely by the dangers faced or the treasures recovered. Its greatest achievements are often quieter: a repaired lantern outpost, a newly mapped route, a rescued traveler, or a settlement reconnected to its neighbors after years of isolation.

Every discovery is recorded.

Every route is documented.

Every expedition adds another page to the growing story of the Spire.

Membership within the Guild is both a privilege and a responsibility. Explorers are expected to uphold the principles expressed within the Guild Oath and to remember that their actions affect not only themselves, but everyone who follows in their footsteps.

The Spire is vast.

No explorer can uncover its secrets alone.

That is why the Guild endures.

### Extract from a Guild Training Manual

The purpose of an explorer is not to reach the furthest frontier.

It is to ensure that those who come after may travel further still.

A single repaired bridge may be worth more than a dozen forgotten trophies.

Record what you learn.

Share what you discover.

Leave the route better than you found it.
