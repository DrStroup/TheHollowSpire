## Canon Stamp Colors

- Black = Standard
- Green = Low Risk / Routine
- Blue = Moderate Difficulty
- Red = High Risk / Dangerous
- Purple = Extreme Risk / Restricted

## Design Principle

Color communicates risk.
Text communicates specifics.
