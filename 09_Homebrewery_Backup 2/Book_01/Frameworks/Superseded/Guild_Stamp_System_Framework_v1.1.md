# Guild Stamp System Framework v1.1

## Locked Rule

Explorer Guild stamps communicate administrative review and contract risk.

These stamps are not faction crests. They are Guild administrative tools used to classify, verify, and communicate contract status.

## Canon Stamp Colors

Black
- Standard
- General certified posting
- Normal contract
- Default administrative approval

Green
- Low Risk
- Routine
- Maintenance work
- Supply transport
- Waystation inspections

Blue
- Moderate Difficulty
- Verified exploration work
- Extended expeditions
- Unverified routes
- Significant travel hazards

Red
- High Risk
- Dangerous
- Hostile creatures
- Active threats
- Known casualties
- Emergency response

Purple
- Extreme Risk
- Restricted access
- Advanced explorers
- Guild authorization required
- Sensitive locations
- Seal-related investigations
- Future Moon Lantern access areas

## Visual Communication Rule

Color communicates risk.
Text communicates specifics.

A player should understand the general danger category before reading the full contract.

## Design Rule

All stamp variants belong to the same administrative family:

- Same lantern symbol
- Same overall structure
- Same stamped ink impression style
- Different ink colors communicate risk category

## Physical Stamp Rule

Guild certification marks should feel like physical ink stamps pressed onto paper.

They should not look like raised emblems, medallions, badges, or faction crests.
