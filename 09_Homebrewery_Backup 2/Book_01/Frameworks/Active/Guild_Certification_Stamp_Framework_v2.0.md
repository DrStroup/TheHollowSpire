# Guild Certification Stamp Framework v2.0

## Status
ACTIVE

## Canon Decision
The Guild color risk system is retired.

Difficulty, danger, and urgency are communicated by the contract text itself, not by stamp color.

## Purpose
The Explorer Guild Certification Stamp communicates only:

- Guild reviewed
- Guild verified
- Contract legitimate

## Design Requirements
The production stamp is:

- A single reusable asset
- A transparent PNG
- A flat ink impression
- Distressed like a clerk's rubber stamp
- Centered on a lantern motif
- Administrative, not heraldic

## Not Allowed
The certification stamp must not function as:

- A difficulty marker
- A risk-tier symbol
- A faction crest
- A wax seal
- A raised emblem
- A medallion
- A badge

## Use
Apply the certification stamp to Guild-reviewed postings, notices, and contracts.
