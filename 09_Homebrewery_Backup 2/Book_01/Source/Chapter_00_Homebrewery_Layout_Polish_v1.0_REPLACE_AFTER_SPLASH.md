# Chapter 0: The Explorer Guild Primer

## 0.1 Welcome to the Hollow Spire

<p class="noIndent">The Hollow Spire is a campaign of exploration, discovery, and restoration set within a vast subterranean world known simply as the Spire.</p>

Beneath the surface lie forgotten settlements, abandoned outposts, ancient ruins, and mysteries that have endured for generations. Dangerous creatures roam the depths. Old routes collapse. New paths emerge. Entire communities struggle to survive in places where light is precious and certainty is rare.

Yet the Hollow Spire is not merely a tale of survival.

It is a story about what explorers leave behind.

Throughout this journey, characters will uncover hidden places, establish new routes, restore lost connections, and help the people who call the Spire home. Every discovery has the potential to change the world. Every repaired lantern, reopened passage, or completed contract leaves the depths a little brighter than before.

The Spire rewards curiosity. Those who venture beyond the safety of familiar roads will uncover forgotten histories, unusual resources, strange creatures, and wonders that few living souls have ever witnessed.

Danger is ever-present. Some challenges can be overcome through courage and determination. Others require preparation, patience, or the wisdom to retreat and return another day. Explorers who survive the longest are rarely the strongest. They are the ones who learn, adapt, and respect the unknown.

This book presents the first region of the Hollow Spire and the settlements, routes, contracts, and discoveries that await within it. Whether your expedition seeks knowledge, adventure, or a chance to leave a lasting mark upon the world, your journey begins here.

<p class="chapter00ClosingLine">The path ahead descends into darkness.</p>
<p class="chapter00ClosingLine">Carry your light well.</p>

## 0.2 The Explorer Guild

<p class="noIndent">No one knows exactly when the Explorer Guild was founded.</p>

Some claim it began as a loose collection of scouts and cartographers who sought to map the safer passages between settlements. Others believe its origins lie in the earliest expeditions into the deeper reaches of the Spire, when survival depended upon cooperation between strangers.

Whatever its beginnings, the Guild has become one of the most respected institutions within the known depths.

Its members come from every walk of life. Couriers, laborers, scholars, hunters, guides, and adventurers alike may choose to take up the Guild's mantle. Some seek knowledge. Some seek purpose. Others simply wish to help those who depend upon the routes that connect the settlements of the Spire.

The Guild serves many roles.

Its members chart unexplored territory, record discoveries, investigate reports from the frontier, assist isolated communities, recover lost knowledge, and maintain the fragile network of routes that allow civilization to endure beneath the earth.

Many explorers spend their entire careers working close to home, helping maintain local roads and responding to nearby contracts. Others descend ever deeper, pursuing mysteries that few dare investigate.

The Guild does not measure success solely by the dangers faced or the treasures recovered. Its greatest achievements are often quieter: a repaired lantern outpost, a newly mapped route, a rescued traveler, or a settlement reconnected to its neighbors after years of isolation.

Every discovery is recorded.

Every route is documented.

Every expedition adds another page to the growing story of the Spire.

Membership within the Guild is both a privilege and a responsibility. Explorers are expected to uphold the principles expressed within the Guild Oath and to remember that their actions affect not only themselves, but everyone who follows in their footsteps.

<p class="chapter00ClosingLine">The Spire is vast.</p>
<p class="chapter00ClosingLine">No explorer can uncover its secrets alone.</p>
<p class="chapter00ClosingLine">That is why the Guild endures.</p>

</div>

## 0.3 Discovery

<p class="noIndent">Every explorer enters the Spire searching for something.</p>

Some seek forgotten knowledge. Others search for new routes, rare resources, missing people, or answers to mysteries that have lingered for generations.

Whatever form it takes, the pursuit of discovery lies at the heart of every expedition.

A discovery is more than simply finding something new.

A discovery is something that changes understanding.

The location of a hidden path, the remains of a lost settlement, an unusual species of cave fungus, evidence of an ancient civilization, or a safer route through dangerous territory may all be considered discoveries. Some discoveries reveal opportunities. Others reveal dangers. The most important discoveries often reveal both.

Not every discovery changes the world immediately. Many begin as a single observation recorded in a field journal or discussed around a settlement hearth. Over time, however, these fragments of knowledge accumulate. Maps become more accurate. Trade routes become safer. Settlements become connected. Mysteries begin to yield their secrets.

The Explorer Guild maintains records of significant discoveries whenever possible, preserving information so that future expeditions may build upon the work of those who came before.

Because of this, every explorer contributes to something larger than themselves.

A traveler who identifies a safer crossing may never meet the hundreds of people who eventually benefit from that knowledge. A scout who documents a dangerous creature may save lives without ever realizing it. A researcher who uncovers a forgotten history may inspire entirely new expeditions years later.

<p class="chapter00ClosingLine">The Spire is too vast for any individual to understand completely.</p>
<p class="chapter00ClosingLine">Its secrets are uncovered one discovery at a time.</p>
<p class="chapter00ClosingLine">Every route mapped. Every mystery investigated. Every question answered. And every new question that follows.</p>

</div>

<div class="guildAside" style="top: 555px;">

<p>The greatest discoveries are not always the rarest.</p>
<p>A hidden chamber may be forgotten again.</p>
<p>A safer road may help travelers for generations.</p>
<p>When recording a discovery, ask a simple question:</p>
<p class="chapter00QuoteLine">"Who benefits from this knowledge?"</p>
<p>The answer often reveals its true value.</p>

## 0.4 Contracts

<p class="noIndent">No explorer can be everywhere at once.</p>

Throughout the Spire, settlements face challenges that require capable hands and willing hearts. A damaged bridge may isolate a village from its neighbors. Dangerous creatures may threaten a trade route. A missing expedition may need to be found before its trail grows cold.

To meet these needs, communities often issue contracts.

A contract is a formal request for assistance. Some are posted publicly by settlements, guild halls, or outposts. Others are carried by messengers or shared through word of mouth. The scale of these requests varies greatly. One contract may involve delivering supplies to a nearby settlement, while another may require an expedition into unexplored territory.

Not every contract involves danger.

Some ask explorers to map a route, document unusual wildlife, recover historical records, investigate rumors, or establish contact with isolated communities. Others call for courage in the face of genuine threats.

Completing a contract benefits more than the individuals involved.

A repaired road may improve travel for years. A recovered map may guide future expeditions. A dangerous creature driven away from a settlement may save countless lives. Even small successes contribute to the broader effort of making the Spire safer, better understood, and more connected.

Rewards vary according to the needs of those offering the contract.

Some communities provide useful resources or materials. Others offer equipment, training, lodging, access to new routes, introductions to important figures, or opportunities that would otherwise remain unavailable. Many explorers find that the trust and goodwill earned through reliable service become valuable rewards in their own right.

<p class="chapter00ClosingLine">The best contracts do more than solve a problem. They create new opportunities for discovery.</p>

</div>

## 0.5 Exploration & Danger

<p class="noIndent">The Hollow Spire rewards curiosity, but curiosity alone is rarely enough to ensure survival.</p>

Every expedition involves risk.

Known routes can become blocked without warning. Underground rivers may flood. Creatures migrate into previously safe territory. Equipment fails. Maps prove incomplete. Even experienced explorers occasionally discover that their understanding of a region was dangerously wrong.

For this reason, successful exploration depends upon preparation as much as courage.

Experienced explorers study available records before setting out. They gather supplies, consult local guides, review route reports, and consider how they will respond if circumstances change. A well-prepared expedition can often overcome challenges that would otherwise prove overwhelming.

Just as important is the ability to recognize danger before it becomes disaster.

Not every obstacle must be confronted immediately. Some threats require additional knowledge, better equipment, stronger allies, or a different approach entirely. There is no shame in retreating from an expedition that has become too dangerous. Many of the most respected explorers in Guild history earned their reputation not through reckless bravery, but through good judgment.

A retreat is not a failure.

Returning with information, observations, and lessons learned often provides the foundation for future success. An explorer who survives to share their findings contributes more to the Guild than one who presses onward without regard for the risks involved.

The Spire is vast, ancient, and largely unexplored.

No expedition can uncover every secret in a single journey.

The most successful explorers understand that exploration is a process of gradual progress. Each map completed, route documented, and challenge overcome builds upon the work of previous expeditions.

<p class="chapter00ClosingLine">Patience is often rewarded. The unknown will still be waiting tomorrow.</p>

</div>

## 0.6 Horror & Wonder

<p class="noIndent">The Hollow Spire is a place of contradictions.</p>

It is a world of forgotten ruins and thriving settlements, of ancient dangers and remarkable beauty. Explorers may spend one day crossing a flooded tunnel haunted by strange sounds and the next standing before a cavern illuminated by thousands of glowing crystals.

Both experiences are equally important.

The Spire is not defined solely by danger, nor solely by discovery. It is the tension between the two that makes exploration meaningful.

There are places within the depths that inspire awe. Vast caverns stretch beyond the limits of sight. Underground forests flourish beneath impossible ceilings. Ancient structures stand as silent reminders of civilizations long vanished. Strange creatures display adaptations found nowhere else in the known world.

Yet wonder often exists alongside uncertainty.

Not every mystery welcomes investigation. Some passages lead to places best avoided. Strange noises echo through abandoned tunnels. Old warnings remain carved into stone long after those who left them have disappeared. Explorers occasionally encounter evidence of events they cannot fully explain.

The unknown can be beautiful.

The unknown can also be frightening.

Experienced explorers learn to respect both.

Fear serves a purpose. It encourages caution, preparation, and careful observation. Recklessness often turns curiosity into tragedy. At the same time, allowing fear to prevent all exploration would mean abandoning the discoveries that continue to improve life throughout the Spire.

The greatest expeditions are remembered not because they avoided danger, but because they faced the unknown with wisdom and determination.

<p class="chapter00ClosingLine">Every descent carries uncertainty. Every discovery carries possibility. And beyond every bend in the tunnel lies the chance to encounter something no one has seen before.</p>

## 0.7 Leave the World Better Than You Found It

<p class="noIndent">The Hollow Spire is filled with mysteries waiting to be uncovered, dangers waiting to be overcome, and wonders waiting to be discovered.</p>

Yet exploration alone is not the purpose of the Explorer Guild.

The Guild exists because people rely upon one another.

Every settlement depends upon routes that must be maintained. Every traveler depends upon those who came before. Every expedition builds upon knowledge earned through the efforts and sacrifices of countless others.

For this reason, explorers are encouraged to follow a simple principle:

<p class="chapter00QuoteLine"><strong>Leave the world better than you found it.</strong></p>

Sometimes this means repairing a damaged bridge.

Sometimes it means documenting a safer route through dangerous territory.

Sometimes it means helping a struggling settlement, sharing valuable knowledge, or ensuring that a hard-learned lesson is not forgotten.

Not every improvement must be grand.

Small actions often have lasting consequences. A lantern relit along an abandoned road may guide travelers for years. A warning marker placed beside a hidden hazard may prevent future tragedies. A single act of kindness may create opportunities that ripple outward long after an expedition has ended.

The greatest explorers are rarely remembered solely for the places they reached.

They are remembered for the changes they made possible.

Throughout this book, you will encounter opportunities to restore outposts, reconnect communities, preserve knowledge, improve routes, assist those in need, and contribute to the future of the Spire.

These opportunities are not distractions from the journey.

They are part of the journey.

Every expedition leaves a mark upon the world.

<p class="chapter00ClosingLine">The question every explorer must answer is simple: What kind of mark will you leave behind?</p>

</div>

## 0.8 Beginning the Journey

<p class="noIndent">Every explorer begins somewhere.</p>

Some begin with careful planning and clear purpose. Others find themselves standing at the threshold of an adventure they never expected to undertake. Regardless of how the journey starts, every expedition begins with a single step into the unknown.

The Hollow Spire contains countless stories.

Stories of lost settlements and forgotten civilizations.

Stories of courage, failure, sacrifice, and discovery.

Stories of people who descended into the depths seeking answers and returned with even greater questions.

The story that follows is one such journey.

As you venture into the Blackwater Warrens, remember the lessons of the Explorer Guild.

Seek discovery.

Respect danger.

Help those in need.

Record what you learn.

And whenever possible, leave the world better than you found it.
