# Chapter 0: The Explorer Guild Primer

## Welcome to the Hollow Spire

<p class="noIndent">The Hollow Spire is a campaign of exploration, discovery, and restoration set within a vast subterranean world known simply as the Spire.</p>

Beneath the surface lie forgotten settlements, abandoned outposts, ancient ruins, and mysteries that have endured for generations. Dangerous creatures roam the depths. Old routes collapse. New paths emerge. Entire communities struggle to survive in places where light is precious and certainty is rare.

Yet the Hollow Spire is not merely a tale of survival. It is a story about what explorers leave behind.

Throughout this journey, characters will uncover hidden places, establish new routes, restore lost connections, and help the people who call the Spire home. Every discovery has the potential to change the world. Every repaired lantern, reopened passage, or completed contract leaves the depths a little brighter than before.

The Spire rewards curiosity, but it also rewards patience. Explorers who survive the longest are rarely the strongest. They are the ones who learn, adapt, and respect the unknown.

<p class="chapter00ClosingLine">The path ahead descends into darkness. Carry your light well.</p>

</div>

## The Explorer Guild

<p class="noIndent">No one knows exactly when the Explorer Guild was founded.</p>

Some claim it began as a loose collection of scouts and cartographers who sought to map the safer passages between settlements. Others believe its origins lie in the earliest expeditions into the deeper reaches of the Spire, when survival depended upon cooperation between strangers.

Whatever its beginnings, the Guild has become one of the most respected institutions within the known depths.

Its members chart unexplored territory, record discoveries, investigate reports from the frontier, assist isolated communities, recover lost knowledge, and maintain the fragile network of routes that allow civilization to endure beneath the earth.

The Guild does not measure success solely by the dangers faced or the treasures recovered. Its greatest achievements are often quieter: a repaired lantern outpost, a newly mapped route, a rescued traveler, or a settlement reconnected to its neighbors after years of isolation.

<div class="chapter00MotifRule"></div>

## Discovery

<p class="noIndent">Every explorer enters the Spire searching for something.</p>

A discovery is more than simply finding something new. A discovery is something that changes understanding.

The location of a hidden path, the remains of a lost settlement, an unusual species of cave fungus, evidence of an ancient civilization, or a safer route through dangerous territory may all be considered discoveries. Some discoveries reveal opportunities. Others reveal dangers. The most important discoveries often reveal both.

Every discovery is recorded. Every route is documented. Every expedition adds another page to the growing story of the Spire.

</div>

<p>It is to ensure that those who come after may travel further still.</p>
<p>A single repaired bridge may be worth more than a dozen forgotten trophies.</p>
<p><strong>Record what you learn. Share what you discover. Leave the route better than you found it.</strong></p>

## Contracts

<p class="noIndent">No explorer can be everywhere at once.</p>

Throughout the Spire, settlements face challenges that require capable hands and willing hearts. A damaged bridge may isolate a village from its neighbors. Dangerous creatures may threaten a trade route. A missing expedition may need to be found before its trail grows cold.

To meet these needs, communities often issue contracts.

A contract is a formal request for assistance. Some are posted publicly by settlements, guild halls, or outposts. Others are carried by messengers or shared through word of mouth. The scale of these requests varies greatly. One contract may involve delivering supplies to a nearby settlement, while another may require an expedition into unexplored territory.

Not every contract involves danger. Some ask explorers to map a route, document unusual wildlife, recover historical records, investigate rumors, or establish contact with isolated communities. Others call for courage in the face of genuine threats.

Completing a contract benefits more than the individuals involved. A repaired road may improve travel for years. A recovered map may guide future expeditions. Even small successes contribute to the broader effort of making the Spire safer, better understood, and more connected.

<p class="chapter00ClosingLine">The best contracts do more than solve a problem. They create new opportunities for discovery.</p>

</div>

## Travel Within the Spire

<p class="noIndent">The Hollow Spire does not surrender distance easily.</p>

Explorers quickly learn that the routes between settlements matter because there are no reliable shortcuts. Roads must be walked. Supplies must be carried. Lantern posts, bridges, maps, and waystations exist because generations of travelers needed them to survive.

Flight is unreliable within the Spire. Creatures capable of soaring elsewhere find their wings heavy, their lift unstable, and their strength quickly spent. Most manage only brief glides, desperate hops, or short bursts of altitude before the weight of the depths pulls them down again.

Teleportation and planar travel fare no better. Spells meant to bend distance, pierce planes, or carry travelers elsewhere fizzle beneath the magical pressure of the Spire. The magic may spark, fold, and collapse, but it does not open a path out.

Summoning may still draw something into the Spire, but it does not provide escape. The summoner remains where they stand.

<div class="chapter00MotifRule"></div>

## Exploration & Danger

<p class="noIndent">The Hollow Spire rewards curiosity, but curiosity alone is rarely enough to ensure survival.</p>

Known routes can become blocked without warning. Underground rivers may flood. Creatures migrate into previously safe territory. Equipment fails. Maps prove incomplete.

Successful exploration depends upon preparation as much as courage. Experienced explorers study available records before setting out, gather supplies, consult local guides, and consider how they will respond if circumstances change.

There is no shame in retreating from an expedition that has become too dangerous. Returning with information, observations, and lessons learned often provides the foundation for future success.

</div>

<p>New recruits occasionally ask why explorers spend so much effort maintaining routes, bridges, maps, lantern posts, and waystations.</p>
<p>The answer is simple.</p>
<p><strong>There are no shortcuts.</strong></p>
<p>Birds do not soar here. Magic cannot carry you away. Roads must be walked.</p>
<p>A repaired road is worth more than a thousand impossible escapes.</p>

## Horror & Wonder

<p class="noIndent">The Hollow Spire is a place of contradictions.</p>

It is a world of forgotten ruins and thriving settlements, of ancient dangers and remarkable beauty. Explorers may spend one day crossing a flooded tunnel haunted by strange sounds and the next standing before a cavern illuminated by thousands of glowing crystals.

Both experiences are equally important. The Spire is not defined solely by danger, nor solely by discovery. It is the tension between the two that makes exploration meaningful.

There are places within the depths that inspire awe. Vast caverns stretch beyond the limits of sight. Underground forests flourish beneath impossible ceilings. Ancient structures stand as silent reminders of civilizations long vanished.

Yet wonder often exists alongside uncertainty. Not every mystery welcomes investigation. Some passages lead to places best avoided. Old warnings remain carved into stone long after those who left them have disappeared.

## Leave the World Better Than You Found It

<p class="noIndent">The Hollow Spire is filled with mysteries waiting to be uncovered, dangers waiting to be overcome, and wonders waiting to be discovered.</p>

Yet exploration alone is not the purpose of the Explorer Guild. The Guild exists because people rely upon one another.

Every settlement depends upon routes that must be maintained. Every traveler depends upon those who came before. Every expedition builds upon knowledge earned through the efforts and sacrifices of countless others.

For this reason, explorers are encouraged to follow a simple principle:

<p class="chapter00QuoteLine"><strong>Leave the world better than you found it.</strong></p>

## Beginning the Journey

<p class="noIndent">Every explorer begins somewhere.</p>

Some begin with careful planning and clear purpose. Others find themselves standing at the threshold of an adventure they never expected to undertake. Regardless of how the journey starts, every expedition begins with a single step into the unknown.

The Hollow Spire contains countless stories: stories of lost settlements and forgotten civilizations; stories of courage, failure, sacrifice, and discovery; stories of people who descended into the depths seeking answers and returned with even greater questions.

The story that follows is one such journey.

As you venture into the Blackwater Warrens, remember the lessons of the Explorer Guild.

Seek discovery. Respect danger. Help those in need. Record what you learn. And whenever possible, leave the world better than you found it.

<p class="chapter00ClosingLine">The tunnels ahead are dark.</p>
<p class="chapter00ClosingLine">The routes are uncertain.</p>
<p class="chapter00ClosingLine">The mysteries are many.</p>
<p class="chapter00ClosingLine"><strong>Your journey begins now.</strong></p>
