# The Hollow Spire — Book 01 Asset Checklist

**Version:** v0.01  
**Scope:** Publication-facing asset readiness for Book 01

## Checklist Fields

- **Source Exists:** The asset exists in the repository.
- **Canon Approved:** The asset matches current project canon.
- **Mechanics Approved:** Any stats, DCs, rewards, or encounter mechanics are table-ready.
- **Art Reference Exists:** A reference image, art prompt, or art brief exists.
- **Viewable:** The asset can be viewed clearly at intended publication scale.
- **Player Safe:** The asset does not reveal DM-only information if intended for players.
- **Homebrewery Ready:** The asset is ready to be inserted into the publication source.
