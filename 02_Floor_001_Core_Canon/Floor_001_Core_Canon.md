## Purpose

Blackwake Prison serves as the onboarding floor for The Hollow Spire.

Its purpose is to:

- Introduce exploration.
- Introduce discovery.
- Introduce recurrence.
- Introduce the Warden.
- Foreshadow the Seven Seals.
- Teach players that knowledge is more valuable than treasure.
- Establish the tone of mystery, survival, and hope.

Blackwake is not a traditional prison.

It is a test.

The prisoners simply do not know it.

---

## Core Floor Summary

Thousands awaken within Blackwake Prison each year.

Most believe they were recently arrested.

Most believe escape will return them home.

Both assumptions are false.

Every prisoner has died before.

Every prisoner has attempted the Spire before.

Their memories have been stripped by the Cycle of Recurrence.

Blackwake is where each new cycle begins.

---

# Region A: Intake Cells

## Purpose

Opening location.

Every campaign begins here.

## Description

Cold stone cells. Iron doors. Dim blue lanterns. Hundreds of occupied cells. Most prisoners awaken simultaneously.

No guards are present.

No explanation is provided.

## Discoveries

Players can discover:

- Scratched warnings.
- Names repeated hundreds of times.
- Maps hidden beneath bunks.
- Messages apparently written in their own handwriting.

## Canon Discovery

One wall contains the phrase:

> We have done this before.

This is the first clue regarding recurrence.

---

# Region B: Cell Blocks

## Purpose

Exploration tutorial.

## Features

- Multiple routes.
- Locked gates.
- Collapsed passages.
- Secret crawlspaces.

## Key NPC

### Old Bram

A prisoner who claims:

> I almost remember.

Bram serves as the first source of recurrence foreshadowing. He remembers fragments of previous cycles, but not enough to explain the truth.

---

# Region C: Processing Hall

## Purpose

First major social hub.

## Features

- Registration desks.
- Confiscation records.
- Destroyed archives.
- Prison manifests.

## Major Discovery

Records dating back hundreds of years list the same prisoner names repeatedly, including names belonging to the player characters.

---

# Region D: Warden's Wing

## Purpose

First mystery location.

## Description

A sealed administrative complex. No living personnel remain.

Despite this:

- Lanterns remain lit.
- Doors occasionally unlock themselves.
- Footsteps can sometimes be heard.

## First Warden Reference

Players discover a plaque:

> Every soul shall be measured.  
> Every debt shall be weighed.  
> Every path shall be witnessed.

No author is named.

---

# Region E: Flooded Service Tunnels

## Purpose

Environmental challenge area.

## Features

- Rising water.
- Hidden passages.
- Dangerous creatures.
- Alternate routes.

## Gameplay Function

This region rewards curiosity, contains several shortcuts, and provides access to secret areas.

---

# Region F: Forgotten Galleries

## Purpose

Lore zone.

## Description

Ancient chambers older than the prison itself.

The stonework predates Blackwake.

The prison was built atop something much older.

## Major Discovery

Murals depict seven luminous gems.

These are the first visual references to the Seven Seals.

No explanation is provided.

## Mural Caption

> The path was sealed.  
> The worthy shall open it.

---

# Region G: The Descent Gate

## Purpose

Floor finale.

## Description

A massive circular gate descends deeper into the Spire.

It is constructed from black stone and silver metal and covered in unknown symbols.

## Floor Boss

### The Chain Warden

A construct left behind to test escaping prisoners.

It is not evil. It is not malicious. It is performing its duty.

## Boss Themes

- Judgment.
- Restraint.
- Worthiness.

## Defeat Conditions

Combat is only one option.

Players may:

- Fight.
- Outsmart.
- Disable.
- Convince.

Multiple solutions are required for publication development.
