# Floor 1 Story Summary — Reconstructed Preservation Draft

Floor 1 introduces explorers to the Hollow Spire and establishes the campaign's core themes: exploration, discovery, survival, curiosity, mercy, justice, ambition, and sacrifice.

Players awaken within the Black Cells. They possess fragmented memories and little understanding of their circumstances. The cells stand open. The prison appears abandoned.

Exploration leads them to the Rotunda, an ancient central hub connecting multiple routes through the floor. Within the surrounding maintenance tunnels they encounter Iron Rats, Broken Custodians, the Silent Jailer, and the Lost Child.

Each encounter introduces a different aspect of the Spire:
- Iron Rats teach that the Spire has ecosystems and recoverable materials.
- Broken Custodians teach that ancient systems still function.
- The Silent Jailer teaches that not all things can be fought.
- The Lost Child teaches that curiosity can reveal paths or mislead.

As players progress they discover evidence that explorers came before them and that portions of the prison remain active despite immense age.

Their journey culminates at the Breach, a vast collapse separating the early prison sectors from the regions beyond. The Breach Crossing serves as the major traversal challenge and climax encounter of Floor 1.

The exact placement of the Flooded Approach and Emberlight remains pending verification from the original story writeups.
