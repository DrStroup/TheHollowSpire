## Protected open mysteries

These are deliberate design unknowns, not errors to auto-correct:
- Whether the breach caused the party's release.
- Exact identity, nature, and final revelation of the hidden creature.
- Keeper / Saint Lantern identity.
- Unrevealed recurrence and Warden truths.

## Closing an issue

Record the deciding source, update affected dependents, verify them, and link a decision ID. An audit report alone is not evidence that the underlying content was corrected.
