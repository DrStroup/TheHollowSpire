## Locked Canon Confirmed

The following are consistent across the repository and should remain locked:

- Book 01 covers **Region 001: The Blackwater Warrens**.
- Book 01 covers **Floors 01–12**.
- Exploration is the primary campaign pillar.
- Contribution and legacy remain secondary pillars.
- The Spire resists teleportation, planar travel, flight-as-escape, and route-bypassing magic.
- Transit Law is locked:
  - No discovery removes the journey.
  - Distance may be shortened. It cannot be ignored.
  - Maps reduce risk. Preparation reduces risk.
  - Only exploration closes the gap.
- Lantern Village is the major Book 01 hub.
- Emberlight is the first settlement.
- Blackwater Run is the horror evacuation sequence.
- Moon Lantern is acquired around Floor 10.
- First Seal is Truth.
- First Seal is acquired by the Book 01 finale.
- Recurrence remains hidden lore and is not directly taught to players.
- Chapter 00 Publication Pass v0.8 is on hold at roughly 75–80% publication readiness.

## Book 01 Structure Audit

### Current Strengths

Book 01 has a strong macro-arc:

> Prisoner → Hope → Loss → Wonder → Discovery → Purpose

The Region 001 floor list is clear:

1. Black Cells
2. Flooded Paths
3. Emberlight Village
4. Drowned Road
5. Shadow Marsh
6. Blackwater Run
7. Lantern Village
8. Veiled Warrens
9. Whispering Deep
10. Moon Lantern Sanctuary
11. Truths Below
12. First Seal

This structure gives Book 01 a strong emotional progression and a clean hub transition from Emberlight to Lantern Village.

### Current Weakness

Only Floor 01 currently has detailed production-ready canon depth. Floor 01 includes a strong purpose lock, location list, NPC integration, encounter chain, discovery design, recurrence handling, and exit logic. Floors 02–12 are currently named and emotionally positioned, but they do not yet have the same development depth.

## Most Critical Missing Component

The critical blocker is:

> **A complete Region 001 Playable Development Spine for Floors 01–12.**

Without this, Book 01 cannot reach publication readiness because Chapter 00 cannot know which rules, procedures, settlements, discoveries, contracts, routes, and warnings must be introduced before play begins.

This spine should be completed before additional Chapter 00 publication polish, Chapter 01 manuscript drafting, or final asset generation.
