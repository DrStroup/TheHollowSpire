## Purpose

Reward optional exploration with a construct threat, tangible recovery, and a future Codex investigation. The Broken Custodian demonstrates that damaged prison systems still function.

Use [BESTIARY-002](../04_Bestiary/BESTIARY-002_Broken_Custodian.md). This placement does not add a second Custodian encounter in Maintenance Hall.

## Recovery and Codex consequence

The encounter's primary recovery is a guaranteed rare Memory Fragment, matching recovery entry 16. It seeds the Broken Custodian investigation: Failed Directive 1/X. X remains undefined; this is a first progression point, not a completed Hunt.

The guarantee belongs to this encounter's recovery opportunity; it does not award an item merely for entering or bypassing the room, and it does not make every Custodian drop a rare fragment. Exact recovery procedure and non-combat resolution remain to be developed.

Other components remain in the bestiary's recovery catalog; they are not an automatic bundle of additional drops.

## Optional branch

No Archive Key, mandatory Codex device, or required progression item is gated behind this encounter. The party returns to the Rotunda. A Memory Fragment found before Codex acquisition remains a recorded recovery for later integration; do not lose or award it twice.
