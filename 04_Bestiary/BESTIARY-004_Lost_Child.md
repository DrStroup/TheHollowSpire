# BESTIARY-004 — The Lost Child

Status: LOCKED v1.0  
Completion: Development Complete + Visualized

## Classification

Phenomenon  
Combat: Impossible  
Recovery: None

## Overview

The Lost Child appears as a small child, usually between six and ten years old, alone, frightened, and moving away from observers.

No records agree on gender, appearance, clothing, or voice.

## Manifestation Rules

- Always seen first, never heard first.
- Never approaches.
- Always appears reachable.
- Disappears before contact.
- Often seen near hidden paths.
- Following does not guarantee safety.
- Has never been captured.

## Effects

- False Trail
- Forgotten Route
- Memory Echo

## Witness Reports

Edrin: "I followed for nearly an hour. The distance never changed."

Sera: "I stopped chasing. The child stopped too."

Halden: "The child never looked back. I think it knew I was there."

Mara: "Nobody asks why it is afraid."
