## Classification

Narrative Entity  
Combat: Never

## Overview

The Silent Jailer is a recurring figure in the Upper Spire. It never attacks, speaks, or initiates interaction. It watches.

## Manifestation Rules

- May appear on any floor.
- May appear to a single observer.
- Multiple observers may witness the same manifestation.
- No observer has witnessed it arrive.
- No observer has witnessed it depart.
- May appear in sealed locations.
- Only one Silent Jailer has ever been observed at a time.

## Warden Ledger Integration

The Silent Jailer observes pivotal moments tied to Mercy, Justice, Ambition, and Sacrifice. It never visibly rewards or punishes behavior.

## Witness Reports
