## Purpose
Teach core Hollow Spire travel philosophy through an in-world Explorer Guild artifact.

## Composition Lock
Asset_004A is a full-width, two-page banner designed to split across Pages 12–13.

Page 12 communicates:
- explorer observations
- discoveries
- field notes
- supplies
- preparation
- the Spire Transit Law

Page 13 communicates:
- route planning
- Blackwater Run navigation
- hazards
- fuel cache notes
- survival planning

## Transit Law Placement
The Spire Transit Law is positioned on the right side of the left page so it is not split directly by the gutter.

## Associated Canon
- Spire Transit Law

- Mapping Culture
- Travel Limitations within the Spire

## Design Notes
The ledger should feel weathered, stained, handled, and used by working explorers. It is a survival tool, not a ceremonial document.
