# Black Cells Art Direction v1.0

## Required / Desired Art Concepts

### A1 — Awakening Cell
A decaying prison cell with a door hanging from one hinge, rusted metal, Iron Rat gnaw marks, damp stone, torn bedding, and no visible jailer.

### A3 — Explorer's Cell
An abandoned explorer pack, journal scraps, and a damaged hand-drawn map inside a cell. The explorer is missing.

### A4 — Iron Rat Nest
Small iron-gnawing rats nesting in broken stone and rusted metal, with damaged bars and burrowed walls.

### A5 — Abandoned Guard Post
A ruined watch post with a broken observation window overlooking the Rotunda, where Nib can be seen far below.

## Visual Tone
Dark, damp, abandoned, and uneasy.

## Avoid
- Overt supernatural imagery.
- Clear Recurrence clues.
- Heroic lighting.
- The escaped entity appearing directly.
