## Framework Conflict Resolutions

### Reward Framework

Resolved.

The older discovery-only reward framing has been replaced with Meaningful Reward Philosophy. Discovery remains important but is only one supported reward category.

Supported reward categories now include:

- Discovery rewards
- Material rewards
- Monster-part rewards
- Training rewards
- Access rewards
- Route and travel rewards
- Settlement and service rewards
- Equipment and tool rewards
- Reputation and relationship rewards
- Narrative progression rewards

### Guild Marks

Resolved.

Guild Marks are removed from canon. Existing references should be flagged and replaced.

### System Reference Standard

Resolved.

Internal balancing may use a modern class-based fantasy roleplaying framework, but publication text should remain system-neutral and should avoid direct proprietary rules references.
