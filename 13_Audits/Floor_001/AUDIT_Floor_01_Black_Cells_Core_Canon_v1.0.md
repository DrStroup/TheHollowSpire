## Corrected Risks

### Risk: The campaign feels like one continuous dungeon

**Resolution:** The new canon defines Floor 01 as the exceptional prison threshold and requires the rest of Book 01 to widen into a subterranean world.

### Risk: Recurrence is explained too early

**Resolution:** The new canon defines recurrence as hidden lore and restricts Floor 01 to rumors, contradictions, and ambiguous phenomena.

### Risk: Floor 01 teaches the wrong theme

**Resolution:** Floor 01 now teaches escape, survival, cooperation, and perseverance rather than recurrence.
