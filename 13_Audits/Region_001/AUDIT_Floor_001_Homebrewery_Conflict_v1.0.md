# AUDIT Floor 001 Homebrewery Conflict v1.0

## Scope

Audit Floor 1 skeleton and current Homebrewery material against active Region 001 canon.

## Result

Floor 1 remains usable as the only explicitly prison-like opening location, but its publication presentation needs careful reframing. The rest of the Spire should not inherit the dungeon/prison tone of the Black Cells.

## No Conflict

- The party may awaken in the Black Cells.
- Floor 1 may include prison architecture, failed prison-maintenance systems, the Broken Custodian, and the Silent Jailer sighting.
- Nib remains the first major guide/survivor NPC.
- The Breach Crossing remains the correct transition out of the opening floor.

## Required Corrections

1. Floor 1 must teach escape, survival, and first exploration, not recurrence.
2. EN-007 Lost Child should be framed as moral unease and mystery, not a recurrence lesson.
3. Any impossible names, records, or repetitions must be clues only. No NPC should explain recurrence.
4. The Breach Overlook should widen the campaign from prison opening to subterranean world.
5. Homebrewery primer must not describe the whole Spire as a megadungeon-prison.

## Current Homebrewery Status

The primer and encounter flow have been patched to align with these corrections. Detailed Floor 1 production can proceed after the Floor 1 canon file is reconciled into active Region 001 canon rather than copied directly from archived recovery files.
