# Publication Audit Rules v1.1

## Status

Active audit standard for Book 01 publication development.

## Core Audit Checks

1. Remove proprietary rules references from publication-facing content.
2. Flag fantasy ruleset-specific terminology when it appears in public manuscript text.
3. Verify Guild Marks are not used as canon, currency, or contract rewards.
4. Flag Discovery Credits and Recovery Caches as deprecated formal reward categories.
5. Verify rewards follow the Meaningful Reward Philosophy.
6. Identify opportunities for monster-part rewards, training rewards, route rewards, settlement rewards, equipment rewards, access rewards, and discovery rewards.
7. Confirm Book 01 references Region 001 / Floors 1-12 where active scope is being described.
8. Confirm Chapter 0 public title references use **The Explorer Guild Primer**.
9. Confirm recurrence, Warden truth, and the Cycle of Recurrence are treated as hidden or GM-facing until the manuscript intentionally reveals them.
10. Confirm publication-facing content is system-neutral unless clearly marked as internal conversion guidance.

## Reward Audit Requirements

For each encounter, contract, bestiary entry, and settlement hook, determine whether the reward is one or more of the following:

- Discovery
- Material
- Monster part
- Training
- Technique
- Special ability
- Equipment
- Access
- Route or travel improvement
- Settlement improvement
- Social trust or reputation
- Narrative progression

If a reward is only money, ask whether a more Hollow Spire-specific reward would better serve the scene.

## Deprecated Reward Language

Flag and replace:

- Discovery Credit
- Discovery Credits
- Recovery Cache
- Recovery Caches
- Guild Marks

Suggested replacements depend on context:

- Discovery Credits -> Discoveries / lore / confirmed findings / guild recognition
- Recovery Cache -> Materials and recoveries / recovered supplies / salvage / relics / evidence
- Guild Marks -> trust / access / support / supplies / training / route permission

## Encounter Conversion Audit

Encounter conversion files should use:

```markdown
## Rewards and Outcomes

### Discoveries

### Materials and Recoveries

### Training or Techniques

### Access, Routes, or Contacts

### Settlement or Narrative Effects
```

Omit irrelevant subsections rather than padding.

## Chapter 0 Audit Requirements

Before drafting new Chapter 0 prose, verify:

- Existing Chapter 0 manuscripts and outlines have been inventoried.
- The public title is **The Explorer Guild Primer**.
- The primer explains exploration, restoration, contracts, discoveries, and meaningful rewards in system-neutral language.
- It does not function as a named commercial rules tutorial.
- It does not introduce Guild Marks.
- It presents contracts as story engines and world-improvement opportunities, not coin dispensers.

## Scope Audit Requirements

Active Book 01 scope:

- Book 01: The Blackwater Warrens
- Region 001
- Floors 1-12

- Emberlight Village

- Lantern Village
