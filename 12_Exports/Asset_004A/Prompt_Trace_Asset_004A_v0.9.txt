
THE HOLLOW SPIRE — ASSET_004A

Create a photorealistic tabletop RPG artifact.

An open, weathered leather explorer journal rests on a dark stone surface illuminated by warm lantern light.

The journal feels personal and heavily used. It belongs to an experienced Hollow Spire explorer. The leather is worn, stained, repaired, and softened from years of travel.

The journal is the primary object in the image.

LEFT PAGE

A mixture of explorer observations and field sketches.

Visible elements include:

- contract reference notes
- personal reminders

The page should feel practical and personal rather than scientific.

TRANSIT LAW

A worn Guild-issued reference sheet is tucked into the center of the journal.

The sheet is unfolded enough that all text is clearly readable.

The sheet reads:

SPIRE TRANSIT LAW

No discovery removes the journey.

No shortcut removes the distance.

Maps reduce risk.

Preparation reduces risk.

Only exploration closes the gap.
