## Locked Decisions

- Discovery is DM-facing and semi-hidden.
- Players do not see Discovery totals or thresholds.
- Lantern brightness is the primary visible expression.
- Discovery thresholds can gate contracts, rumors, trust, access, and information.
- Discovery is not a currency and not a traditional reputation score.
- Discovery rewards curiosity and meaningful exploration.
- Discovery should not be awarded publicly as visible points.
