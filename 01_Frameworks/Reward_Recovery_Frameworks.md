# Reward and Recovery Frameworks

## Hollow Spire Economy Rule v0.01

XP = Objectives  
Loot = Recovery  
Rewards = Reputation / Progression

## Discovery Reward Framework v0.02

Only two exploration reward categories exist:

### Discovery Credit

Represents:
- Knowledge
- Achievement
- Recognition
- Progress

Future uses:
- Lantern growth
- Guild standing
- Settlement reputation
- Training
- Contracts
- Unlocks

### Recovery Cache

Represents:
- Physical resources
- Evidence
- Materials
- Artifacts
- Supplies

Recovery Caches must draw from the Master Recovery Registry.

## Discovery Assets

Discovery Assets are not rewards.

Examples:
- Journals
- Explorer notes
- Maps
- Witness reports
- Graffiti
- Contracts
- Rumors
- Sketches
- Historical records

Discovery Assets may grant:
- Discovery Credits
- Recovery Cache locations
- Future encounter hooks
- Contract leads
