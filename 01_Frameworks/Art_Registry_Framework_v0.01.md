# Art Registry Framework v0.01

All artwork is tracked independently of encounter, bestiary, NPC, and module development.

## Art Categories

- Creature Art
- Construct Art
- NPC Art
- Narrative Entity Art
- Phenomena Art
- Discovery Asset Art
- Encounter Art
- Map Art
- Relic Overlay Art

## Completion Terms

### Development Complete
Requires mechanics, lore, discovery assets, recoveries, and registry audits.

### Visualized
Requires approved reference image.
Applies to creatures, constructs, NPCs, narrative entities, and phenomena.

### Publication Complete
Requires all registered art assets produced, integrated, and audited.
