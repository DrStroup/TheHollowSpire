# System Neutral Publication Framework v1.1

## Locked Decisions
- Published Hollow Spire content is system-neutral.
- Avoid trademarked or proprietary game-system references in publication text.
- Guild Marks are removed from canon.

## Meaningful Reward Philosophy

Rewards should provide meaningful advancement beyond currency.

Supported reward categories:
- Discoveries and lore
- Monster materials (Iron Rat Whiskers, spores, shells, etc.)
- Training and mentorship
- New techniques and special abilities
- Equipment and expedition tools
- Route unlocks and travel improvements
- Settlement restoration benefits
- Social reputation and trusted contacts
- New contracts and quest-givers
- Narrative progression

Currency may exist in the setting but should rarely be the most exciting reward.
