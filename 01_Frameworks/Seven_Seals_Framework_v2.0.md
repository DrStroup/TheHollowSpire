# Seven Seals Framework v2.0

## Status
CANON LOCKED

## Canon Decision

The Seven Seals are not merely relics.

They are the seven judgments by which the Warden measures a soul.

Each Seal represents a virtue that must be understood, demonstrated, and ultimately chosen by the candidate.

## The Seven Seals

| Seal | Virtue |
|---:|---|
| Seal I | Truth |
| Seal II | Mercy |
| Seal III | Justice |
| Seal IV | Sacrifice |
| Seal V | Ambition |
| Seal VI | Perseverance |
| Seal VII | Hope |

## Seal I — Truth

Book 01 culminates in the First Seal of Truth.

Truth is the foundation of the Explorer Guild's worldview:

- Accurate maps
- Accurate records
- Accurate observations
- Honest reporting
- Reliable routes

## Campaign Use

The Seven Seals should be foreshadowed before they are explained.

Guild teachings, explorer sayings, ancient inscriptions, and Warden-related artifacts may quietly echo the Seven Seals without naming them directly.

## Asset_004 Guidance

Asset_004 — Common Truths of the Spire should use seven numbered truths.

Each truth may subtly align with one of the Seven Seals, but the player-facing artifact should not explicitly explain the full Seal structure.
