# Explorer Map Directives

Explorer maps are not copies of player maps.

They are records of experience, not records of reality.

## Rules

- Explorer maps are hand-drawn field documents.
- They are rough approximations.
- Distances may be wrong.
- Areas not reached are omitted.
- Areas explored under stress may be incomplete.
- Explorers invent names.
- Personal commentary is expected.
- Different explorers reveal different portions of the same floor.
- Maps should feel like field journals, not cartographic reproductions.

Every explorer has Reach.

Some areas are fully explored.  
Some areas are partially explored.  
Some areas are abandoned.  
Some areas are never reached.
