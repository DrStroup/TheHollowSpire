# The Hollow Spire — System Neutral Publication Framework v1.0

## Status

Locked for Book 01 publication development unless superseded by a later explicit canon or publication framework update.

## Purpose

This framework updates Hollow Spire publication rules before the next manuscript audit pass. It replaces older system-specific assumptions and removes Guild Marks from canon.

## Rule 01 — System Neutral Publication

The Hollow Spire shall be written as a system-neutral fantasy exploration campaign.

Internal development may use a modern 2024 fantasy d20 reference framework for encounter balancing, progression pacing, creature design, and playtesting.

Published manuscripts shall avoid dependence on any proprietary game system wherever practical.

The goal is that readers can adapt the material to their preferred fantasy roleplaying system with minimal effort.

## Rule 02 — No Proprietary Rules or Setting Names

Published content shall avoid direct references to copyrighted, trademarked, or proprietary game products, settings, monsters, classes, spells, rules modules, and brand names.

Use generic terminology instead, such as:

- fantasy roleplaying game
- character level
- adventuring party
- exploration campaign
- fantasy bestiary
- core rules
- game master
- creature statistics
- spellcaster
- divine magic
- arcane magic
- exploration check

Internal design notes may reference the development framework indirectly, but publication manuscripts shall remain system-neutral.

## Rule 03 — Internal Development Reference Standard

For development purposes only, Hollow Spire design work assumes a modern class-based fantasy d20 framework with bounded progression, streamlined exploration mechanics, and low-complexity encounter design.

This reference is used only for pacing and balance.

Published manuscripts should never require ownership of a specific rules product.

## Rule 04 — Currency Reduction Philosophy

The Hollow Spire prioritizes discovery, contribution, restoration, and exploration over wealth accumulation.

Traditional currency may exist in the world when required for realism, but it should rarely serve as the primary motivation for contracts.

Rewards should primarily take the form of:

- discoveries
- knowledge
- access
- reputation
- restored routes
- guild recognition
- unique tools
- exploration opportunities
- settlement improvements
- story progression
- safer passage
- rare maps
- expedition support
- historical records

## Rule 05 — Guild Marks Removed

Guild Marks are removed from canon.

Previous references to Guild Marks should be considered deprecated.

Contracts should instead provide rewards such as:

- access to new regions
- restoration of services
- equipment loans
- rare maps
- expedition support
- exploration permits
- historical records
- unique discoveries
- settlement trust
- narrative advancement

No Hollow Spire subsystem should rely on Guild Marks.

## Rule 06 — Discovery First Design

Every major reward should answer at least one of the following questions:

- What can the players learn?
- What can the players restore?
- What new place can the players reach?
- What mystery can the players uncover?
- How does the world become better because they were here?

If a reward only increases wealth, consider replacing it with a discovery-based reward.

## Rule 07 — Audit Implication

The next audit pass should no longer be framed as a conversion from one named ruleset to another named ruleset.

The correct audit target is:

> Convert all mechanics and terminology into system-neutral publication language while preserving the internal modern 2024 fantasy d20 reference framework for development use only.

## Locked Outcomes

- Publication language is system-neutral.
- Proprietary game and setting names are avoided in publication-facing text.
- Guild Marks are removed from canon.
- Contract rewards are discovery-first rather than currency-first.
- Existing Hollow Spire narrative canon remains authoritative unless contradicted by later audit findings.
