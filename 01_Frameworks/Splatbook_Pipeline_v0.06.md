## Contract Rule

Contracts originate from Contract Issuers.

Contract Issuers may exist:
- In settlements
- On main floor maps
- On sub-maps
- Within factions
- Within hidden communities

A contract may only be created after:
1. The issuer exists.
2. The objective exists.
3. The reward exists.
4. The completion condition exists.
