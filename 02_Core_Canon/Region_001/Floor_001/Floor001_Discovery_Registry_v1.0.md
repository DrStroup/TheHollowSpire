# Floor 001 — Discovery Registry v1.0

## D-01 — The Prison No Longer Functions

### Introduced In
Zone 01 — Black Cells

### Required
Yes.

### Player-Facing Understanding
The prison is no longer capable of holding prisoners.

### Evidence
- Doors destroyed or hanging open.
- Locks rusted through.
- Iron Rat damage.
- Abandoned guard post.
- Failed prison systems.
- No visible authority remains.

### Protected Canon
This discovery must not reveal Recurrence, prior descents, the true nature of the Spire, or the party's larger imprisonment.

### Design Function
Establishes the opening theme:

Freedom Does Not Feel Like Freedom.
