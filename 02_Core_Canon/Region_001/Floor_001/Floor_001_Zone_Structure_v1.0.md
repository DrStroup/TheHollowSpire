# THE HOLLOW SPIRE

## Book 01 — Region 001
## Floor 001 — The Black Cells
## Zone Structure v1.0 (Locked)

Core Design Principle

Floor 1 exists to teach the exploration loop of The Hollow Spire.

Players should naturally learn:

Explore → Discover → Learn → Return → Progress

without requiring explicit instruction.

Zone Purposes

Zone 1 — Black Cells

Purpose:
Awakening.

Players discover:

* Prison doors destroyed.
* Prison systems dead.
* First contradictory explorer map.
* Path toward the Rotunda.

Zone 2 — Rotunda

Purpose:
Orientation Hub.

Players discover:

* Nib.
* Lantern Stability.
* Route selection.
* The first safe rally point.

The Rotunda functions as the central hub of Floor 1.

Zone 3 — Forgotten Block

Purpose:
Optional Exploration Branch.

Players discover:

* Additional explorer records.
* Contradictions.
* Environmental storytelling.
* Optional discoveries and rewards.

Zone 4 — Maintenance Hall

Purpose:
System Investigation.

Players discover:

* Damaged prison systems.
* Damaged Codex.
* Lantern infrastructure.
* Evidence that parts of the prison still function.

Zone 5 — Hidden Records Vault (HA-3)

Purpose:
Archive Discovery.

Players discover:

* Archive fragments.
* Codex significance.
* Historical records.
* Archive Key.

The key obtained here allows progression beyond the locked route in the Shattered Corridor.

Zone 6 — Shattered Corridor

Purpose:
Containment Breach Investigation.

Initially inaccessible.

After obtaining the Archive Key, players return and gain access.

Players discover:

* Catastrophic structural damage.
* Evidence of the escaped entity.
* Signs that something forced its way through prison defenses.

Zone 7 — Last Barrier

Purpose:
Final Defense Line.

Players discover:

* The final prison defenses.
* The scale of the containment failure.
* The last intact obstacle between the prison interior and the breach.

Zone 8 — The Breach

Purpose:
Floor Climax.

Players discover:

* The prison is not the world.
* The prison exists inside the larger Spire.
* The route into Floodwater Passages.

System Integration

Floor Theme

Freedom Does Not Feel Like Freedom.

The prison is broken.

The cells are open.

The guards are gone.

Something escaped.

The players are free.

Yet nothing feels safe.
