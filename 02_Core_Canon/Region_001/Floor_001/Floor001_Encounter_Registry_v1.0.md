# Floor 001 — Encounter Registry v1.0

## E-01 — Iron Rat Nest

### Zone
Zone 01 — Black Cells

### Area
A4 — Iron Rat Nest

### Purpose
First hostile encounter.

### Encounter Role
Low-threat introductory combat.

### Teaches
- The prison remains inhabited.
- Environmental creatures contribute to prison decay.
- Some hazards are ecological rather than evil.
- Iron Rats explain damage to doors, hinges, and weakened walls.

### Notes
This encounter should not feel like the main threat of Floor 1. It is a survival-space encounter, not a story boss.
