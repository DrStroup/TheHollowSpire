# THE HOLLOW SPIRE

## Book 01 — Region 001

## Floor 001 — The Black Cells

## Zone 01 — Black Cells Content Pass v1.0

### Purpose

The Black Cells serve as the opening zone of The Hollow Spire.

The zone teaches:

* Movement
* Observation
* Exploration
* Environmental storytelling

The zone does not teach:

* Lantern Stability
* Hunts
* Codex systems
* Recurrence
* Major prison lore

Those systems are introduced later.

### Desired Emotional Flow

Confusion

↓

Isolation

↓

Curiosity

↓

Unease

↓

Discovery

↓

Relief upon reaching the Rotunda

### Major Discovery

D-01

The Prison No Longer Functions

Players should quickly understand:

* Doors no longer secure prisoners.
* Guard stations are abandoned.
* Systems have failed.
* No authority remains.

### Area A1 — Awakening Cell

#### Purpose

Opening scene.

#### Description

The party awakens inside a deteriorated prison cell.

Observations:

* Cell door hanging from one hinge.
* Extensive rust damage.
* Iron Rat gnaw marks.
* Damp stonework.
* Torn bedding.
* Water stains.

Most important observation:

Nobody released them.

Nobody is guarding them.

Nobody is coming.

#### Discovery

D-01

The Prison No Longer Functions.

### Area A2 — Collapsed Cell Row

#### Purpose

First exploration space.

#### Description

A partially collapsed block of adjacent cells.

Features:

* Flooded sections.
* Fallen masonry.
* Accessible prison cells.
* Minor survival loot.

#### Possible Loot

* Rope fragments.
* Rusted tools.
* Empty lantern shells.
* Old travel packs.
* Minor crafting materials.

### Area A3 — Explorer's Cell

#### Purpose

Introduce contradictions.

#### Description

One cell clearly belonged to an explorer rather than a prisoner.

Contents:

* Damaged backpack.
* Journal fragments.
* Explorer Map #17.

#### Discovery

The explorer is missing.

No body.

No skeleton.

No signs of departure.

No explanation.

#### Contradiction

Explorer Map #17 does not perfectly match the surrounding prison layout.

Differences are minor and explainable.

Players should assume the map is simply outdated.

### Area A4 — Iron Rat Nest

#### Purpose

First hostile encounter.

#### Description

A cluster of Iron Rats has burrowed through several prison walls.

Players learn:

* Why many doors have failed.
* Why sections of the prison are collapsing.
* That the prison remains inhabited.

#### Encounter

Iron Rats.

Low-threat introductory combat encounter.

### Area A5 — Abandoned Guard Post

#### Purpose

Transition to the Rotunda.

#### Description

A ruined security station overlooking the central chamber beyond.

Features:

* Scattered records.
* Extinguished lantern.
* Broken observation window.
* View into the Rotunda.

#### Discovery

Players observe the first living figure encountered in the campaign:

Nib.

Far below.

Moving through the Rotunda.

### Exit Condition

The party reaches the Rotunda.

### Zone Theme

The prison is not secure.

The prison is not maintained.

The prison is not understood.

Yet something still lives here.
