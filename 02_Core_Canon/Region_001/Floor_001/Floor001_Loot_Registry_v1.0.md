# Floor 001 — Loot Registry v1.0

## Zone 01 — Black Cells

### Loot Philosophy
Loot in the Black Cells should feel like survival salvage, not treasure.

### Possible Finds
- Rope fragments.
- Rusted tools.
- Empty lantern shells.
- Old travel packs.
- Minor crafting materials.
- Journal fragments.
- Explorer Map #17.

### Restricted
Do not introduce major magic items, Guild rewards, Codex upgrades, or Lantern Stability mechanics in Zone 01.

## Zone 03 — Forgotten Block

EN-005, F5: Memory Fragment — Rare, guaranteed primary encounter recovery. Use existing master recovery entry 16; do not create a second item identity. It seeds Failed Directive 1/X, with completion requirements unresolved. Recovery procedure remains a content-development task.

The optional branch contains no mandatory device or progression key.

## Zone 04 — Maintenance Hall

Damaged Codex — mandatory Floor 01 acquisition (CAN-004). Exact activation/repair and first-use procedure are open. Existing Maintenance Tools remain environmental salvage; the EN-005 relocation does not move them to F5.

## Zone 05 — Hidden Records Vault (HA-3)

Archive Key — required for the locked Shattered Corridor route. Archive context expands the Codex's significance; detailed chip placement and quantities require the zone content pass.
