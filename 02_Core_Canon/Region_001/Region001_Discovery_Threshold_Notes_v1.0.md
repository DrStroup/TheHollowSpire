# Region 001 Discovery Threshold Notes v1.0

## Status

Initial Region 001 implementation notes.

## Use In Book 01

Discovery thresholds may be used to gate:

- Emberlight tasks.
- Lantern Village trust.
- Guild attendant contract availability.
- Hunt leads.
- Registry cache hints.
- Hidden route information.
- Settlement requests.
- Moon Lantern-related foreshadowing.

## Example Region 001 Threshold

A Guild attendant may withhold a task until the party has demonstrated enough familiarity with the area.

DM-facing:
Requires 50 total Discovery.

Player-facing:
"Nothing for you right now. Spend a little more time in the area and check back."

After the threshold is crossed:
"Actually... something came in while you were away. You might be ready for this."

## Early Book 1 Guidance

Do not overexplain the system.

Players should experience Discovery as:

- The Guild trusting them more.
- Lanterns glowing brighter.
- More dangerous information becoming available.
- Settlements responding to them differently.

## Floor 001 Guidance

Floor 001 should introduce Discovery through observation and contradiction, not through visible scoring.

Relevant early discoveries:
- D-01: The Prison No Longer Functions.
- Explorer Map #17 contradiction.
- First evidence that previous explorers came here.
- First evidence that the prison is inhabited.
