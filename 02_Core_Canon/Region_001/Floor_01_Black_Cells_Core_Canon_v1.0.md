# Floor 01 — Black Cells Core Canon v1.0

**Project:** The Hollow Spire  
**Book:** Book 01 — The Blackwater Warrens  
**Region:** Region 001 — The Blackwater Warrens  
**Floor:** 01  
**Status:** Active Canon  
**Purpose:** Production anchor for Floor 01 manuscript, encounter, map, NPC, bestiary, and Homebrewery conversion work.

---

## 1. Canon Position

The Black Cells are the opening threshold of Book 01. They are the only early section that should feel overtly prison-like. Their job is to begin with confinement and end by widening the campaign into a living subterranean world.

Floor 01 is not designed to explain the Hollow Spire. It creates questions, teaches survival, introduces the first ally, and moves the party from captivity into exploration.

The players should leave this floor thinking:

> What is this place, and how do we keep going?

They should not leave with a clear explanation of recurrence, the Warden's cosmic purpose, or the full nature of the Spire.

---

## 2. Player-Facing Identity

To the players, the Black Cells are a ruined prison threshold beneath the world. The place is old, broken, damp, and partially abandoned. It contains cells, rusted mechanisms, collapsed passages, failed maintenance systems, and hints that other people have passed through before.

The rest of the campaign should not feel like one continuous dungeon. Floor 01 is the confined prologue before the larger world opens.

---

## 3. Designer-Facing Identity

The Black Cells are a controlled opening experience that introduces:

- escape under uncertainty
- exploration through partial information
- first contact with another survivor
- discovery-based rewards
- the idea that records, maps, and memories may contradict one another
- awe through scale rather than exposition

Any deeper metaphysical truths remain hidden lore.

---

## 4. Core Themes

### Primary Theme: Escape

The party begins trapped and must choose movement, cooperation, and courage over paralysis.

### Secondary Theme: Survival

The characters have little context and limited safety. Progress comes through observation, caution, and improvisation.

### Regional Theme Connection: Perseverance

Floor 01 expresses perseverance in its simplest form: stand up, find a way out, and keep moving even when nothing makes sense.

### Book Theme Connections

- **Exploration:** The Rotunda teaches route choice.
- **Contribution:** Helping Nib establishes that survival below is communal.
- **Legacy:** Old marks, tallies, and route records show that others endured before.
- **Perseverance:** The floor begins with captivity and ends with the choice to continue.
- **Truth:** The floor provides clues, not answers.

---

## 5. Emotional Arc

1. **Disorientation** — The party awakens with no reliable explanation.
2. **Unease** — The prison feels broken but not empty.
3. **Trust** — Nib becomes the first living connection.
4. **Tension** — The Silent Jailer sighting shows that not every threat can be fought.
5. **Wonder** — The Breach reveals the world beyond the cells.
6. **Determination** — The party exits toward the Flooded Paths.

---

## 6. Major Locations

### The Awakening Cells

Separate or nearby cells where the player characters first wake. Doors may be damaged, open, jammed, or partially unlocked depending on the table's desired opening pressure.

The area should emphasize sensory detail: cold stone, iron, dripping water, old scratches, and the unsettling absence of explanation.

### The Rotunda

The first major hub. It teaches that the Spire is navigated through choices rather than linear rooms. Multiple exits, collapsed routes, old marks, and partial records should make the party feel like they are interpreting a place rather than clearing it.

### Collapsed Maintenance Niche

Nib's introduction point. This location should be dangerous enough that helping him matters, but not so complex that it delays the early flow.

### Iron Rat Colony

A small ecological hazard that demonstrates that the Black Cells are inhabited by scavengers and adapted creatures, not just placed monsters.

### Custodian Workhall

The Broken Custodian encounter area. The prison's maintenance systems continue to function poorly, dangerously, and without understanding current conditions.

### The Silent Gallery

A corridor, overlook, or ruined station where the Silent Jailer may be seen. This is a sighting only. It must not become a combat encounter.

### The Lost Child Passage

A strange route where the party may witness or follow the Lost Child phenomenon. It should remain ambiguous and player-interpretable.

### The Breach Crossing

The transition point from prison threshold to the wider Hollow Below. This is the floor's climactic traversal challenge and the best place for the first major awe beat.

---

## 7. Major NPC

### Nib

**Role:** Guide, survivor, scout, first ally  
**Source:** `05_NPCs/NPC-001_Nib.md`

Nib is helpful, observant, talkative, and brave when it matters. He should make the Spire feel survivable without solving the floor for the party.

Nib's function is to introduce the social truth of the campaign:

> No one descends alone.

Nib should never explain the full nature of the Hollow Spire, recurrence, or the Warden. He knows routes, rumors, survival habits, and local fears. He does not know the ultimate truth.

## 10. Discovery Design

Floor 01 should reward observation. Discoveries should feel useful, eerie, and incomplete.

Possible discovery categories:

- cell graffiti
- prisoner tallies
- repeated symbols
- broken route marks
- old explorer tags
- chapel/prayer alcove records
- custodian mechanism notes
- witness accounts
- route sketches
- memory echoes

Discovery text should avoid definitive metaphysical explanations. It is acceptable for discoveries to raise contradictions.

---

## 11. Recurrence Handling

Recurrence is hidden lore.

Floor 01 may contain phenomena that players later reinterpret, but no NPC, read-aloud text, sidebar, or primer passage should plainly teach recurrence as a known fact.

Allowed presentation:

- rumors
- contradictory marks
- repeated names without explanation
- déjà vu-like details
- witness accounts that could be wrong
- strange echoes

Disallowed presentation:

- direct explanation that souls die and return
- NPC exposition about the Warden recycling candidates
- player-facing labels such as "Cycle of Recurrence"
- treating recurrence as a known regional rule

---

## 12. Warden and Ledger Handling

The Warden Ledger may exist as a DM-facing campaign tool, but Floor 01 should not present it as a player-facing system.

Floor 01 may create ledger-relevant choices, especially around:

- helping Nib
- responding to helpless or strange figures
- choosing mercy under pressure
- choosing whether to preserve or destroy records
- abandoning or aiding others during escape

These choices should affect later consequences quietly.

---

## 13. Awe Beats

### Minor Awe: The Rotunda / Chain Mechanism

A large vertical or circular mechanism suggests that the prison is only one piece of a much larger structure.

### Major Awe: The Breach Overlook

At the end of the floor, the party sees beyond the prison: flooded paths, distant lights, black water, carved stoneworks, and hints of settlements or routes far below.

This moment is essential. It tells the table:

> The campaign is opening into a world, not continuing through a hallway.

---

## 14. Exit State

Floor 01 ends with the party leaving the Black Cells and moving toward:

`Floor 02 — Flooded Paths`

By this point, the party should have:

- escaped immediate confinement
- made at least one meaningful choice about helping another person
- learned that maps and marks matter
- encountered at least one unsolved mystery
- glimpsed the wider subterranean world
- gained a reason to keep going
