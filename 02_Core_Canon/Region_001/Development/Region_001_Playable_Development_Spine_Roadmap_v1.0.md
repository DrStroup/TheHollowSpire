# Region 001 Playable Development Spine Roadmap v1.0

**Project:** The Hollow Spire  
**Book:** Book 01 — The Blackwater Warrens  
**Region:** Region 001  
**Purpose:** Define the next development milestone required before Book 01 can become publication-ready.

---

## North Star

Book 01 must teach players that The Hollow Spire is not a dungeon to clear. It is a subterranean world to explore, map, survive, repair, and understand.

Every floor should reinforce at least one of the following:

- Exploration creates progress.
- Routes matter.
- Maps reduce danger but do not replace travel.
- Settlements change based on player choices.
- Contribution and legacy matter.
- Truth is earned by going deeper, not by being told answers.

---

## Development Order

### Phase 1 — Regional Spine Lock

Create one standardized development sheet for each floor, Floors 01–12.

Each sheet should answer:

- What does this floor teach?
- What does this floor threaten?
- What does this floor reveal?
- What route does this floor open, damage, or close?
- What can players leave better than they found it?
- What does this floor add to Chapter 00 requirements?

### Phase 2 — Settlement and Route Web

Develop the functional connection between:

- Emberlight Village
- Blackwater Run
- Lantern Village
- Moon Lantern Sanctuary
- backtracking routes unlocked by the Moon Lantern
- optional restored routes, bridges, lamps, ferries, hoists, boats, lifts, and safe camps

This phase should make travel meaningful without reducing the campaign to road-only movement.

### Phase 3 — Contract and Discovery Architecture

For each floor, define:

- 3–5 discoveries
- 2–4 contract hooks
- 1–2 recovery opportunities
- 1 major route or access reward
- at least one choice that affects a later settlement, route, or NPC

Player-facing contracts should not show discovery point values. DM-facing development may assign discovery value separately.

### Phase 4 — Encounter Skeletons

After the playable spine is complete, build encounter chains for Floors 02–12 following the Floor 01 model.

Each floor should have a default teaching flow, but should not become a linear railroad.

### Phase 5 — Manuscript Conversion

Only after Phases 1–4 should the book move into chapter manuscript conversion.

Recommended order:

1. Chapter 01 — The Black Cells
2. Chapter 02 — Flooded Paths
3. Chapter 03 — Emberlight Village
4. Chapter 04 — Drowned Road
5. Chapter 05 — Shadow Marsh
6. Chapter 06 — Blackwater Run
7. Chapter 07 — Lantern Village
8. Chapter 08 — Veiled Warrens
9. Chapter 09 — Whispering Deep
10. Chapter 10 — Moon Lantern Sanctuary
11. Chapter 11 — Truths Below
12. Chapter 12 — First Seal
13. Return to Chapter 00 for final content-driven update

## Floor-by-Floor Priority Notes

### Floor 01 — Black Cells

Use as the template floor. It already defines purpose, locations, encounter chain, discovery design, recurrence handling, and exit logic.

### Floor 02 — Flooded Paths

Needs the first true exploration procedure test: water levels, routes, hazards, map uncertainty, and safe passage creation.

### Floor 03 — Emberlight Village

Needs settlement identity, NPC roles, first contract board, first contribution opportunities, and emotional attachment before the later evacuation.

### Floor 04 — Drowned Road

Needs route-based travel gameplay. This should demonstrate Transit Law in play: distance can be shortened but not ignored.

### Floor 05 — Shadow Marsh

Needs wilderness-in-the-Spire identity, environmental navigation, supply pressure, and foreshadowing of Blackwater Run.

### Floor 06 — Blackwater Run

Needs horror evacuation design. This should be a chase/survival sequence, not a conventional boss fight.

### Floor 07 — Lantern Village

Needs major hub design: guild services, routes, NPC factions, contract growth, safety limits, and visible consequences from earlier choices.

### Floor 08 — Veiled Warrens

Needs post-hub exploration escalation and a reason to return to Lantern Village with new information.

### Floor 09 — Whispering Deep

Needs Truth foreshadowing, unreliable records, memory echoes, route contradictions, and Moon Lantern anticipation.

### Floor 10 — Moon Lantern Sanctuary

Needs relic acquisition structure. The Moon Lantern should unlock new exploration options without functioning as fast travel.

### Floor 11 — Truths Below

Needs backtracking payoffs, Moon Lantern route reveals, and the strongest pre-finale truth tests.

### Floor 12 — First Seal

Needs Book 01 finale, Truth Seal trial, regional consequence resolution, and transition hooks into Book 02.

- What should be hidden until play?
