## What this floor teaches

Movement, observation and survival salvage in the Black Cells; route choice and social trust in the Rotunda; tangible rewards for optional exploration in Forgotten Block; prison-system investigation and mandatory Codex acquisition in Maintenance Hall; returning with new access knowledge through HA-3.

The working campaign balance from the June 11 user direction is approximately 50% exploration/discovery, 25% social and 25% combat, subject to later revision. This is a design emphasis, not a room quota or guarantee that players choose combat.

## What this floor threatens

Iron Rats provide introductory ecological combat. The optional Broken Custodian adds a construct threat. Damaged infrastructure and the Breach supply traversal pressure. The Silent Jailer remains non-combat; the Lost Child remains ambiguous.

## What this floor reveals

The prison is broken but inhabited. Earlier explorers left conflicting records. Some systems still function. The Breach reveals a larger subterranean world. Recurrence, the Warden's purpose, and definitive breach causation remain hidden or unresolved.

## What players can leave better

Cooperation can improve Nib's immediate survival prospects. Preservation of recovered records can support later understanding and research. A specific repair/restoration opportunity for this floor has not yet been designed; do not claim a functioning contribution loop before the Maintenance Hall pass supplies one.
