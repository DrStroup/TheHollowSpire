## Canon Position

Region 001 is the opening region of The Hollow Spire. It introduces the campaign through exploration, settlement life, contract work, mapping, discovery rewards, and the first major artifact tied to the Seven Virtues.

The player-facing experience should emphasize hope, legacy, awe, curiosity, contribution, and the feeling that civilization persists in impossible places.

The deeper truths of prison, testing, judgment, and recurrence may exist in meta-canon, but they should almost never be presented plainly to players in Region 001.

---

## Player-Facing Identity

Region 001 is not primarily an escape story.

It is the beginning of a descent.

The party begins as unknown explorers who learn how the Spire is traveled, mapped, repaired, remembered, and inherited by those who come after.

---

## Book 01 Progression Role

By the end of Book 01, the party should have become recognized explorers rather than world-saving heroes.

They should understand:

- How contracts work.
- Why maps matter.
- How settlements depend on explorers.
- Why discovery is a form of inheritance.
- How artifacts can reshape exploration.
- That the Spire contains far more life and history than expected.
