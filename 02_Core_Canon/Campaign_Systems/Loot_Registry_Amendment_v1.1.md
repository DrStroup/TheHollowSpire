# Loot Registry Amendment v1.1

## Guild Research & Crafting Fields

Loot entries may include the following additional fields:

```text
Research Use:
Crafting Use:
Contribution Use:
Required Specialist:
Discovery Threshold:
Recipe Links:
Settlement Use:
Codex Link:
```

## Anti-Gold Rule

Loot should not become generic currency.

Avoid universal conversion systems such as:

```text
30 common loot = 3 potions.
```

Prefer specific recipes:

```text
Lesser Lantern Draught
Requires:
- 10 Iron Rat Whiskers
- 2 Glowcap Mushrooms
- 1 vial treated lantern oil
- Discovery Threshold 50
- Guild Alchemist
```

## Material Identity Rule

Materials should retain identity.

Iron Rat Whiskers should matter because they come from Iron Rats, not because they are worth a generic number.

## Crafting Gate Rule

Crafting requires:

1. Discovery Threshold.
2. Specific Materials.
3. Specialist Access.
