# THE HOLLOW SPIRE

## Explorer Map Contradictions Registry v1.0

### Status
Locked Framework.

### Core Rule

A contradiction should always have at least one mundane explanation available.

If players immediately conclude:
'Reality is broken.'

The contradiction is too strong for early campaign use.

Desired reaction:

'Huh. That's odd.'

### Contradiction Categories

#### Category I — Cartographic Contradictions

The map itself disagrees with reality.

Examples:
- Missing room.
- Extra room.
- Corridor doesn't exist.
- Door is elsewhere.
- Staircase moved.

Desired player reaction:
'Bad map.'

#### Category II — Explorer Contradictions

Different explorers report different things.

Examples:
- Safe route vs unsafe route.
- Active shrine vs destroyed shrine.

Desired player reaction:
'Somebody is wrong.'

#### Category III — Naming Contradictions

Locations possess multiple names.

Examples:
- Cell Block C.
- The Forgotten Wing.
- East Holding.

Desired player reaction:
'Names changed.'

#### Category IV — Historical Contradictions

Records disagree.

Examples:
- Different arrival dates.
- Different expedition counts.

Desired player reaction:
'Records are unreliable.'

#### Category V — Route Contradictions

Travel reports disagree.

Examples:
- Route travel times differ.
- Safe route becomes collapsed.

Desired player reaction:
'Conditions changed.'

#### Category VI — Presence Contradictions

People appear absent where evidence suggests they should exist.

Examples:
- Explorer pack with no explorer.
- Recent journal with missing author.

Desired player reaction:
'What happened to them?'

#### Category VII — Legacy Contradictions

Reserved for later books.

Examples:
- Explorer credited for discoveries they never made.
- Statues depicting incorrect individuals.
- Archives referencing impossible events.

Desired player reaction:
'Something deeper is wrong.'

### Contradiction Strength Scale

#### Tier 1 — Mundane
Examples:
- Wrong room.
- Wrong door.
- Wrong name.

Book 1 primary contradiction tier.

#### Tier 2 — Suspicious
Examples:
- Missing explorer.
- Conflicting records.
- Route inconsistencies.

Book 1–2.

#### Tier 3 — Disturbing
Examples:
- Impossible timelines.
- Duplicate journals.
- Repeated names.

Book 3–5.

#### Tier 4 — Reality Threatening
Examples:
- Same explorer centuries apart.
- Contradictory existence records.

Book 6–8.

### Campaign Distribution Guidance

Book 1:
80% Tier 1
20% Tier 2

Book 2:
60% Tier 1
40% Tier 2

Book 3:
Tier 3 begins appearing.

Book 6+:
Tier 4 becomes possible.

### Design Rule

Contradictions are a long-form foreshadowing system.

They should support:
- Recurrence foreshadowing.
- Historical uncertainty.
- Explorer culture.
- Discovery rewards.
- Warden questions.
- Late-game revelations.

without confirming protected canon.
