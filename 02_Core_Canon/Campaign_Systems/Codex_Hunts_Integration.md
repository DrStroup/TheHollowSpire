# Explorer's Codex and Hunts — one system

Status: Accepted system relationship; detailed mechanics incomplete.
Audience: GM / designer.
Decision: CAN-002 in [DECISIONS.md](../../00_Project_Status/DECISIONS.md).

The Explorer's Codex and Hunts are one integrated system. “Hunt” remains a term for a type of investigation or activity within that system, not an independent progression system to build alongside it.

The user's prior correction established the merger. Recovered June 10–11 decisions also establish mandatory Floor 01 acquisition and evidence-led progression; they do not supply a complete integration specification. Preserve existing observations, samples, chips, and discovery records; do not invent ranks, currencies, rewards, or automatic unlock mechanics.

## Existing supported behavior

The Codex is an ancient containment-record device covering fauna, flora, anomalies, phenomena, and lost expeditions. Exploration can reveal entries, evidence, samples, chips, and archive records.

Hunt activities are discovered through exploration and evidence. Existing types are Observation, Harvest, Containment, and Extermination. Record their evidence and stage alongside the related Codex entry.

An ordinary contract to hunt a creature is not automatically a formal Codex Hunt. Do not force the formal Hunt system onto every hunting request.

## Mandatory Floor 01 introduction

CAN-004 restores the earlier user decision: acquire the damaged device in Maintenance Hall on Floor 01. HA-3 expands its significance and archive context. The device is a required core-system introduction, not a reward for visiting the optional Forgotten Block. The older Floor 02 preference is superseded.

Acquisition is established; the exact activation, repair, and first-use procedure is still open. The Maintenance Hall content pass must make the mandatory acquisition reliable and explain available functionality without assuming a fully working device.

Encountering entities and gathering evidence can open or advance related Codex/Hunt entries. Codex Chips can unlock unknown entries/Hunts or advance existing ones. Fractions such as 1/9, 1/4, and 1/2 were recalled as examples, not a universal threshold table.

The specific accepted Floor 01 seed is the Forgotten Block Memory Fragment: Broken Custodian investigation, Failed Directive 1/X. X remains open. If recovered before the device, retain the evidence for later integration; recording the same finding in the Codex does not grant another Discovery award.

## One record, several views

A finding can appear in a floor checklist, encounter checklist, and Codex/Hunt record, but counts once toward Discovery. The same occurrence must use the same award key. “Hunt” may remain an activity tag; it does not imply a second independent point award or progression total.

## Still open

- Exact entry/stage schema and completion requirements.
- How chips, samples, and Hunt completion interact.
- Device activation/repair and first-use procedure after the established Floor 01 acquisition.
- Reward tuning and any migrated legacy records.

These are open implementation questions, not permission to restore separate Hunt and Codex systems.
