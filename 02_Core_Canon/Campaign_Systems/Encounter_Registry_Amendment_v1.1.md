
# Encounter Registry Amendment v1.1

## New Fields

Discovery Potential
- None
- Minor
- Moderate
- Major
- Exceptional

Related Discovery IDs

## Rule

Encounters do not automatically award Discovery.

Encounter
→ Observation
→ Understanding
→ Discovery
→ Discovery Points

## Discovery Potential Scale

None: 0
Minor: 1-2
Moderate: 3-5
Major: 5-10
Exceptional: 10+

## Example

E-001 Iron Rat Nest
Discovery Potential: None
Related Discovery IDs: ED-001

E-117 Crystal Shrine
Discovery Potential: Major
Related Discovery IDs: ED-017
