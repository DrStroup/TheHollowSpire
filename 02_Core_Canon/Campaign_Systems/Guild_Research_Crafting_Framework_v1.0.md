# THE HOLLOW SPIRE

## Guild Research & Crafting Framework v1.0

### Status

Locked Framework.

### Purpose

This framework defines how loot, creature materials, salvage, Discovery thresholds, Guild expertise, and crafting interact without turning loot into gold with extra steps.

The goal is to create a Hollow Spire reward loop where exploration produces knowledge and materials, and the Explorer Guild uses both to create new opportunities.

---

# Core Principle

Discovery unlocks knowledge.

Specific materials enable crafting.

Guild expertise turns both into useful tools.

Loot should not become a generic currency.

---

# Anti-Gold Rule

If loot can be converted into a universal value and spent on anything, it has become gold.

Avoid:

```text
10 Iron Rat Whiskers = 50 value
20 Scrap = 100 value
Spend 150 value on any item.
```

This is gold with extra bookkeeping.

Use instead:

```text
Lesser Lantern Draught
Requires:
- 10 Iron Rat Whiskers
- 2 Glowcap Mushrooms
- 1 Lantern Eel Oil Sac
- Discovery Threshold: 50
- Guild Alchemist access
```

The materials should tell a story.

---

# Core Reward Loop

```text
Explore
    ↓
Discover
    ↓
Unlock Knowledge
    ↓
Gather Specific Materials
    ↓
Contribute To Guild Research
    ↓
Craft Specialized Rewards
    ↓
Explore Further
```

This keeps the campaign centered on exploration rather than wealth.

---

# Discovery Thresholds And Crafting

Discovery thresholds may unlock:

- Recipes.
- Guild specialists.
- Crafting stations.
- Alchemical formulas.
- Smithing techniques.
- Codex-based research.
- Archive permissions.
- Rare material identification.
- Relic stabilization.

Discovery proves the party has enough understanding, trust, or regional experience to access deeper Guild work.

---

# Material Requirements

Crafting should require specific materials, not generic loot points.

Good examples:

- Iron Rat Whiskers.
- Lantern Eel Scales.
- Glowcap Mushrooms.
- Deepsteel Fragments.
- Crystal Shrine Dust.
- Saint-Lantern Ash.
- Archive Cores.
- Moon Lantern Residue.

Bad examples:

- 30 common loot.
- 100 salvage points.
- 500 material value.

Generic exchanges should be avoided unless used only for mundane supplies.

---

# Guild Contribution Model

The party does not simply sell materials.

They contribute materials to Guild research, settlement projects, and specialist crafting.

Possible outcomes:

- Potions.
- Repairs.
- Lantern upgrades.
- Training access.
- Route restoration.
- Settlement improvements.
- Codex research.
- Companion care.
- Specialized gear.
- Relic stabilization.

This reinforces the Explorer Guild as a living institution rather than a shop.

---

# Crafting Access Model

Crafting requires three components:

## 1. Discovery Threshold

The party must have demonstrated enough exploration and understanding.

## 2. Specific Materials

The party must recover relevant components through exploration, hunts, salvage, encounters, or Codex work.

## 3. Specialist Access

The party must have access to someone or something capable of crafting the item.

Examples:

- Guild Alchemist.
- Guild Smith.
- Lanternkeeper.
- Archivist.
- Codex technician.
- Settlement artisan.
- Relic conservator.

---

# Example: Guild Alchemist

## Lesser Lantern Draught

Availability:
Discovery Threshold 50.

Requires:
- 10 Iron Rat Whiskers.
- 2 Glowcap Mushrooms.
- 1 vial of treated lantern oil.

Specialist:
Guild Alchemist.

Effect:
Creates 3 Lesser Lantern Draughts.

Purpose:
Early exploration support.

---

## Blackwater Tonic

Availability:
Discovery Threshold 100.

Requires:
- 3 Blackwater Reed Bundles.
- 2 Lantern Eel Scales.
- 1 sealed flask from Floodwater Passages.

Specialist:
Guild Alchemist.

Effect:
Temporary resistance or advantage against selected blackwater hazards, depending on final ruleset.

Purpose:
Supports deeper exploration of flooded routes.

---

# Example: Guild Smith

## Deepsteel Explorer Staff

Availability:
Discovery Threshold 250.

Requires:
- 3 Deepsteel Fragments.
- 2 Crystal Shrine Shards.
- 1 Archive Core.
- 1 suitable staff base.

Specialist:
Guild Smith.

Effect:
Allows the player to combine up to three effects from the appropriate rare item table, subject to final balance rules.

Purpose:
Creates a meaningful late-Region or mid-campaign crafting goal.

---

# Example: Route Restoration

## Repair Collapsed Lantern Bridge

Availability:
Discovery Threshold 75.

Requires:
- 12 salvage beams.
- 6 ancient fittings.
- 4 treated rope bundles.
- Lanternkeeper support.

Specialist:
Lanternkeeper or settlement crew.

Effect:
Unlocks safer route access and reduces future expedition risk.

Purpose:
Turns loot into legacy and route improvement.

---

# Reward Types Created By Guild Research

Guild research may produce:

- Potions.
- Field tools.
- Lantern upgrades.
- Route repairs.
- Settlement infrastructure.
- Training opportunities.
- Codex upgrades.
- Companion support items.
- Special weapons or implements.
- Relic stabilization procedures.

---

# Repeat Adventure Guidance

Repeat expeditions may be encouraged when rare materials are needed.

However, repeated harvesting should not become mindless grinding.

Good repeat adventure:

- Players return to a dangerous route because rare Lantern Eels migrate there only under certain conditions.
- Players revisit a restored shrine to collect a limited material after solving its mystery.
- Players pursue a Hunt to obtain a specific component.

Bad repeat adventure:

- Players kill common creatures repeatedly until enough generic currency drops.

---

# Common Material Guidance

Common materials may support:

- Mundane supplies.
- Low-tier potions.
- Repairs.
- Early crafting.
- Settlement contribution.
- Codex sample completion.

Common materials should not become a universal exchange rate.

---

# Rare Material Guidance

Rare materials should be:

- Named.
- Region-specific.
- Discovery-linked.
- Often tied to Hunts, Wonders, or hidden zones.
- Used in meaningful recipes.

A rare material should make players remember where it came from.

---

# Relationship To Loot Registry

This framework amends the Loot Registry.

Loot entries may now include:

- Research Use.
- Crafting Use.
- Contribution Use.
- Required Specialist.
- Discovery Threshold.
- Recipe Links.

---

# Relationship To Discovery

Discovery unlocks knowledge and trust.

Materials unlock execution.

This creates two independent gates:

```text
Discovery Threshold = You know enough.
Specific Materials = You explored enough.
Specialist Access = Someone can make it.
```

---

# Relationship To The Codex

Codex progress may identify:

- Which materials are useful.
- What creature components do.
- Which samples complete recipes.
- Which companion care items are possible.
- Which materials are safe or dangerous to use.

Codex completion can reveal recipes that the Guild did not previously understand.

---

# Relationship To Settlements

Settlements may request contributions.

Examples:

- Emberlight needs Iron Rat Whiskers for anti-rust treatment.
- Lantern Village needs Glowcap oil for lamp maintenance.
- St. Lantern's Vigil needs relic fragments for restoration.

Such contributions may unlock:

- Trust.
- Training.
- Safe lodging.
- Route access.
- Settlement upgrades.
- New NPC services.

---

# Final Design Rule

A crafted reward should make future exploration more interesting.

Not merely easier.

Interesting.
