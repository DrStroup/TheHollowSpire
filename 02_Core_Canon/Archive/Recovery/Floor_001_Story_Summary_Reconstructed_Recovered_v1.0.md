# Floor 001 Story Summary Reconstructed
## Blackwake Prison

**Project:** The Hollow Spire  
**Book:** Book 01 — The Light Below  
**Version:** v1.0 Canon Lock Draft  
**Repository Location:** `02_Floor_001_Core_Canon/Floor_001_Story_Summary_Reconstructed.md`

---

The player characters awaken in the Intake Cells of Blackwake Prison with no reliable memory of how they arrived. The cells are old, cold, and strangely maintained. The prison appears abandoned by its authorities, yet the mechanisms still function as if unseen hands continue the work.

The first discoveries teach the core truth of the floor without explaining it directly: names repeat across the walls, prisoner records list impossible dates, and several clues imply that the characters have attempted this escape before.

As the characters leave the Intake Cells and move into the Cell Blocks, they meet survivors whose reactions range from fear to opportunism. Old Bram remembers fragments of previous cycles. Lysa the Runner understands the prison routes better than most. Jorek Ashhand sees the collapse of order as a chance to rule. Sister Vael interprets Blackwake as a place of judgment rather than punishment.

The Processing Hall reveals the prison’s impossible history. The same names appear across centuries. The characters may discover records that suggest Blackwake has processed them many times before.

The Warden’s Wing introduces the unseen intelligence behind the prison. The Warden is not named directly. Instead, the characters find statements of judgment, debt, and witness. The wing should feel watched, but not haunted in a simple way.

The Flooded Service Tunnels offer environmental exploration and alternate routes. The tunnels establish that Blackwake is not merely a prison complex but a structure built over older foundations.

The Forgotten Galleries confirm this deeper history. Murals show seven luminous gems sealing a path downward. These are the first visual foreshadowing of the Seven Seals.

The floor culminates at the Descent Gate, where the Chain Warden tests whether the characters are worthy to descend. The Chain Warden is not a villain. It is a threshold guardian. Combat is possible, but publication development should preserve alternate resolutions through cleverness, sacrifice, mercy, or demonstrated worthiness.

By the end of Floor 001, the characters should understand only three things clearly:

1. Blackwake is not what it appears to be.
2. They have been here before.
3. The only path forward is down.
