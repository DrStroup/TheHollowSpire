WORKING THEORY

The creature may have slipped through a flaw in its containment.

This displacement may involve unusual interactions with space, time, routes,
or the deeper systems of the Spire.

Reports may appear contradictory.

Different explorers may record seemingly impossible sightings.

This uncertainty contributes to myths, legends, and false explanations.

DROWNED KING CONNECTION

The Drowned King may function as a cultural explanation for events that do
not fit together cleanly.

Settlements may blame the Drowned King for disasters that actually have
multiple causes.

KEEPER CONNECTION

The Keeper's true responsibility may have been tracking, understanding,
monitoring, and responding to this anomaly.

The Keeper's failure is not simply personal weakness.

The Keeper forgot the purpose while preserving the tradition.
