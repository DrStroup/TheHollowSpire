THE HOLLOW SPIRE
BOOK 01 — THE BLACKWATER WARRENS

Black Cells Containment Lock v1.0
Status: Provisional Canon Lock

LOCKED DIRECTION

1. THE BLACK CELLS ARE MORE THAN A NORMAL PRISON
The opening prison should feel wrong.

Cells, occupants, architecture, and surviving evidence should suggest
that the facility served a purpose beyond conventional incarceration.

The exact purpose remains unknown.

2. THE PARTY'S FREEDOM COINCIDES WITH THE BREACH
The breach and the party's release occur at the same time.

However, it is not yet canonically established that the breach directly
released the party.

This ambiguity should be preserved.

3. THE BREACH IS A SYMPTOM
The breach is the first major visible sign that something within Region 001
has changed.

It should be treated as evidence of a larger problem rather than a complete
explanation.

4. THE HIDDEN THREAT REMAINS CONNECTED
The threat responsible for the escalating crisis, Blackwater Run, and the
need for the Moon Lantern may ultimately be connected to the same pattern
of failures that produced the breach.

Exact causation remains intentionally unresolved.

5. ENDGAME COMPATIBILITY
This lock is intended to remain compatible with future revelations regarding:
- The Warden
- The greater prison structure
- Recurrence
- The true purpose of the Hollow Spire

No Book 01 content should explicitly reveal those answers.

DESIGN GOAL

Players should leave Floor 1 asking:

"What happened here?"

rather than

"How do I escape?"
