THE HOLLOW SPIRE
Lantern Oil Framework v1.0

STATUS: PROVISIONALLY LOCKED

CORE PRINCIPLE

The limiting factor is not how much oil a party can carry.
The limiting factor is how long Lantern Oil remains stable away from a valid source.

LANTERN OIL STABILITY

Oil degrades as explorers move farther from stable sources.

Valid Sources:
- Settlements
- Lantern Shrines
- Lantern Outposts
- Sanctuaries

These locations restore oil stability.

STANDARD GUILD LANTERN

Guild-Issue Reservoir Capacity:
10 Stability

Book 01 baseline expectation.

TRAVEL

Traveling to a new Zone Map consumes 1 Stability.

Previously illuminated routes may be traversed freely during the same expedition.

Returning to a valid source restores expedition state.

RARE OILS

Examples:
- Refined Lantern Oil (+1 Stability)
- Moon-Touched Oil (+2 Stability)
- Saint's Reserve (+3 Stability)
- Deepwell Distillate (+5 Stability)

Rare oils are valuable exploration rewards.

EXPLORATION PRESSURE

Players should solve range problems through:
- Route discovery
- Shortcut discovery
- Shrine restoration
- Outpost restoration
- Reservoir upgrades

rather than carrying unlimited quantities of oil.
