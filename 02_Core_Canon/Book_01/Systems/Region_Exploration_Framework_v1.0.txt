
THE HOLLOW SPIRE
Region Exploration Framework v1.0

Floors are composed of multiple Zone Maps.
Zone Maps are the primary exploration unit.

Lantern Oil:
- 1 Oil per new Zone Map traveled.
- Previously illuminated routes may be traversed freely during the same expedition.
- Returning to a settlement, shrine, or safe outpost resets expedition state.

Encounter Depth:
1 Zone = 20%
2 Zones = 40%
3 Zones = 60%
4 Zones = 80%
5+ Zones = Escalating Risk

Encounter Categories:
- Discovery
- Fauna
- Environmental
- Social
- Threat
- Wonder

Discoveries are finite.
Encounters are infinite.

Each Floor should contain:
- Primary Route
- Optional Routes
- Discovery Opportunities
- Hunt Opportunities
- Story Progression
- Return Path

Nib introduces:
- Light
- Lantern Oil
- Mapping
- Route Safety
- Exploration Principles
