Hunts Framework — compatibility reference
Status: Integrated into the Explorer's Codex; not a separate progression system.
Authority: 02_Core_Canon/Campaign_Systems/Codex_Hunts_Integration.md
Decision: CAN-002, recorded 2026-09-16.

Hunts are discovered through exploration. Existing activity types remain Observation, Harvest, Containment, and Extermination. They can lead to mysteries, submaps, caches, and discoveries within the integrated Codex system.

Detailed stage and reward mechanics remain open. Ordinary hunting contracts are not automatically formal Codex Hunts.
