LANTERN RESERVOIRS v1.0

Reservoir upgrades permanently increase Stability.

Examples:

Reinforced Reservoir
+1 Stability

Silver-Walled Reservoir
+2 Stability

Saint-Lantern Reservoir
+3 Stability

Moon-Touched Reservoir
+5 Stability

Reservoirs are prestigious rewards.

Sources:
- Explorer Guild rewards
- Expedition rewards
- Ancient relics
- Rare crafting projects

Reservoir upgrades should be uncommon and meaningful.
