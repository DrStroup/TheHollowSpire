LOCKED DIRECTION

The entity currently referred to as the Monster Under the Bed is not a traditional villain.

The creature is:
- partially uncontained
- disoriented
- moving through Region 001
- poorly understood by both settlements and explorers

The creature functions as a dark mirror of the player characters.

Both are explorers moving through a world they do not fully understand.

The creature's movements contribute to:
- the breach
- route failures
- Blackwater Run
- disappearing explorers
- regional instability

The creature is not confirmed to be malicious.

Its actions may be harmful without being intentionally destructive.

The creature should not be treated as the sole explanation for all mysteries in Region 001.

The larger mystery remains the Hollow Spire itself.

The creature illuminates the mystery.
It does not replace it.
