THE HOLLOW SPIRE
BOOK 01 — THREAT DEVELOPMENT FILE

Working Threat: The Monster Under the Bed
Version: v1.0 Provisional Development Lock

FUNCTION
This creature is the ticking clock of Book 01.

It should be present from Act 1 through indirect contact, environmental signs, and escalating consequences.

It is not merely a final boss.

EARLY PRESENCE OPTIONS
Floor 1:
- Something moves beyond the flooded prison passages.
- The party sees impossible damage or displaced stone.
- A corridor collapses after something passes through a lower channel.
- A sound is heard beneath the cells that no prisoner can explain.

Floor 2:
- Unrecognizable tracks or drag marks.
- Floodwater moves against expected current.
- A lantern station has gone dark from below.
- Bodies or abandoned supplies imply sudden flight.

Floor 3:
- Emberlight locals mention missing travelers.
- Residents blame the Drowned King.
- The party learns that broken routes are becoming more common.

Floor 4:
- Lantern oil stations fail in a pattern.
- A contract sends the party to repair or relight a route.
- Evidence suggests the threat is moving, not stationary.

Floor 5:
- Blackwater rumors intensify.
- Explorers vanish.
- The party finds signs that the blamed cause does not match the evidence.

Floor 6:
- Blackwater Run culminates as a horror escape sequence.
- The party survives the consequence, not the explanation.

LATER ESCALATION
Floors 7–9:
- Lantern Village and St. Lantern's Vigil reveal social consequences.
- Pilgrims, explorers, and villagers all have partial explanations.
- The Drowned King remains the common scapegoat.

Floors 10–12:
- Contradictory evidence points toward the hidden truth.
- The Moon Lantern becomes necessary.
- The Keeper's neglect is revealed.
- The threat can finally be perceived or understood.

DESIGN NOTES
The creature may not be evil in a simple sense.

It may be:
- ancient
- territorial
- disturbed
- attracted by failing lantern networks
- responding to a neglected duty
- misunderstood by settlements

Do not finalize the creature's exact nature until the floor skeleton and final Book 01 climax are developed.
