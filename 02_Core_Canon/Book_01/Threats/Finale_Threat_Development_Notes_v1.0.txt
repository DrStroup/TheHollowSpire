THREAT DEVELOPMENT NOTES

Current preferred structure:

Book 01:
- Introduction
- Escalation
- Blackwater Run
- Lantern Village threat
- Physical confrontation
- Temporary resolution

Future Books:
- Additional encounters
- Additional evidence
- Expanded understanding
- Relationship evolution

Design Principle:

The creature should remain larger than a single boss encounter.

The encounter resolves the crisis.

It does not resolve the mystery.

Potential Long-Term Direction:

The creature may remember encounters the party does not.

This concept remains provisional and tied to broader recurrence-compatible
development.
