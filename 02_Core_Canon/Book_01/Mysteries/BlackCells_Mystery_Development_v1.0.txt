MYSTERY DEVELOPMENT NOTES

Working Concept:

The Black Cells may have functioned as a containment system rather than a
traditional prison.

single obvious classification.

The facility should feel abandoned long before the campaign begins.

The Hollow Spire may represent part of a larger prison mechanism or system.

THIS IS NOT CONFIRMED CANON.

Preserve ambiguity until later books.
