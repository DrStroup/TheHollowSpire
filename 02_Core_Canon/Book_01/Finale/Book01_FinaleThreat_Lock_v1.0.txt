THE HOLLOW SPIRE
BOOK 01 — THE BLACKWATER WARRENS

BOOK 01 FINALE THREAT LOCK v1.0
Status: Provisional Canon Lock

LOCKED DIRECTION

1. THE PARTY PHYSICALLY CONFRONTS THE THREAT

Book 01 concludes with a genuine confrontation against the entity currently
known as the Monster Under the Bed.

This is a real encounter.
This is a real threat.
This is a real climax.

Players should feel they have earned a victory.

2. LANTERN VILLAGE IS GENUINELY SAVED

The threat to Lantern Village is real.

The party's actions successfully prevent immediate disaster.

The victory should not feel false or invalidated.

3. THE MOON LANTERN CHANGES THE ENCOUNTER

During the confrontation, the Moon Lantern reveals information, truth,
history, memory, pathways, or evidence that alters the creature's behavior.

The exact revelation remains intentionally undefined.

4. THE CREATURE DOES NOT REQUIRE DESTRUCTION

Current preferred direction:

The creature departs rather than being permanently destroyed.

Its departure is a consequence of new information, revelation,
recognition, understanding, memory, or altered purpose.

Exact cause remains open.

5. THE PARTY'S INTERPRETATION

The party may reasonably conclude:

- they defeated it
- they drove it away
- they solved the problem
- they saved Lantern Village

These conclusions are valid from the party's perspective.

6. FUTURE REAPPEARANCE

The creature remains available for future books.

Future encounters may suggest that the creature remembers previous
interactions with the party.

The meaning of this should remain unresolved until much later development.

7. THEMATIC PURPOSE

Book 01 teaches Truth.

The party does not resolve the creature's full story.

They learn enough truth to stop the immediate crisis and continue exploring.
