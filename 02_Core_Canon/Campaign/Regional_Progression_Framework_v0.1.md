# Regional Progression Framework v0.1

## Status

Recovery draft.

This document exists to establish the campaign-scale framework for a 100-floor Pathfinder 1e megadungeon.

## Known Campaign Progression

- Floor 1: Campaign begins at character level 3.
- Floor 89: Characters are expected to be approximately level 20.
- Floors 90–100: Epic-level exploration and endgame content.

## Known Book 01 Scope

Book 01 covers Region One:

- Floors 1–10
- Blackwater Warrens
- The Black Cells
- Floodwater Passages
- Outpost Emberlight
- The Long Dark
- Lantern Village
- Veiled Warrens
- Moon Lantern Sanctuary
- Return Home
- Mysteries of Region One
