# Explorer Creed v1.0

## No One Descends Alone

The Spire is vast.

No explorer will uncover all of its secrets.

No expedition will chart every path.

No lantern will burn forever.

Yet every journey matters.

Map one passage.

Repair one waylantern.

Rescue one lost traveler.

Deliver one journal home.

Leave one refuge safer than you found it.

The measure of an explorer is not how deep they descend.

It is whether those who follow walk a brighter road because they were there.

**No One Descends Alone.**
