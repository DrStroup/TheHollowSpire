# Core World Law — The Spire Resists Escape v1.0

## Canon Status
LOCKED CANON. This is a hard design and worldbuilding rule for The Hollow Spire.

## Core Principle
The Hollow Spire resists escape.

The deeper one travels, the more strongly the Spire presses against unnatural movement, shortcut magic, planar departure, and attempts to bypass routes, distance, danger, or descent.

## Flight
Sustained flight is generally not possible within the Spire.

Flying creatures are not erased from the setting, but their relationship to flight is tragic and altered. Wings grow heavy. Air behaves wrongly. Lift fails at the moment it should hold. Many winged creatures can only hop, flutter, glide, or achieve brief bursts of height before the Spire pulls them back down.

Flying creatures should often feel mournful, frustrated, diminished, or adapted around the loss of true flight. Their inability to soar is part of the emotional texture of the world.

## Teleportation and Fast Travel
Teleportation and magical fast travel generally fail within the Spire.

Spells, rituals, artifacts, portals, class features, or similar effects that would instantly relocate creatures across meaningful distance either fizzle, collapse harmlessly, or fail to find purchase under the magical weight of the Spire.

This includes, but is not limited to:

- Short-range teleportation.
- Long-range teleportation.
- Gate effects.
- Recall effects.
- Portal travel.
- Fast-travel shortcuts.
- Route-bypassing magic.

## Planar and Dimensional Travel
Planar travel, dimensional escape, and similar reality-piercing effects do not function normally inside the Spire.

Attempts to leave the Spire by stepping into another plane, crossing through extradimensional shortcuts, or opening a path beyond the world fail under the Spire's metaphysical pressure.

The Spire is not merely underground. It is sealed by a reality-weight that refuses easy departure.

## Summoning Distinction
Summoning may still function when it brings something into the Spire or manifests a temporary presence, but it does not allow the summoner or party to leave, bypass distance, or create reliable fast travel.

## Gameplay Purpose
This law protects the core experience of The Hollow Spire:

- Routes matter.
- Maps matter.
- Supply lines matter.
- Backtracking matters.
- Lantern posts matter.
- Bridges, lifts, ferries, roads, and waystations matter.
- Discovery changes the world because travel cannot be skipped.

The Hollow Spire is a campaign of descent, route-making, and restoration. Anything that removes the need to travel through the world undermines the central design of the game.

## Publication Language
The rule should usually be expressed diegetically rather than as a dry restriction.

Preferred phrasing:

> Roads must be walked.
>
> Wings do not soar here.
>
> The Spire does not let go.

## Chapter 0 Placement
This law should appear in Chapter 0 as an Explorer Guild training excerpt or similar diegetic artifact titled **Travel Within the Spire**.

It should be presented as common Explorer Guild knowledge before Chapter 1 begins.
