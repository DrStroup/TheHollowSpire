# Common Truths of the Spire v1.1

## Canon Status
Locked for Asset_004B publication use.

## Ownership / Cultural Source
The Common Truths are not Explorer Guild doctrine. They are pilgrim and community wisdom preserved in Lantern Village and associated with travelers, lantern bearers, explorers, and those who remember the lost.

## Public Text
The public-facing Common Truths are:

Record the journey.

Paper doesn't breathe.

Nothing is without consequence.

Remember what we leave behind.

Seek ever-farther horizons.

One more step.

A lantern shines brightest in the dark.

## Internal Seal Alignment
The public text may echo the Seven Seals structure, but the seal/virtue names must not appear in the Asset_004B image or public-facing page artifact.

Internal order only:
Truth, Mercy, Justice, Sacrifice, Ambition, Perseverance, Hope.
