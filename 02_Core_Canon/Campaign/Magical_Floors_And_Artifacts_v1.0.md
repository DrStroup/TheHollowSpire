# Magical Floors and Artifacts v1.0

## The Spire Is Not Conventional

The Hollow Spire is not a normal dungeon made only of stone corridors and chambers.

Many floors are magical, impossible, or reality-defying environments.

## Example Magical Floor Concepts

Examples include:

- Endless seas.
- Areas completely submerged in liquid.
- Breathable underwater regions unlocked through artifacts.
- Reality-distorted floors.
- Secret environments revealed only after acquiring specific relics.
- Regions that change meaning after backtracking.

## Artifact-Gated Exploration

Artifacts are an important part of exploration progression.

They may allow characters to:

- Breathe in otherwise impossible environments.
- Reveal hidden routes.
- Unlock sealed passages.
- Access submerged regions.
- Return to earlier floors with new options.
- Discover secrets that were always present but previously unreachable.

## Design Guidance

Artifacts should not simply be treasure.

Major artifacts should change how the party explores the Spire.

They should create reasons to return, remap, restore, and rediscover previous locations.
