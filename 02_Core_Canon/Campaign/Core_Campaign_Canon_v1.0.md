# Core Campaign Canon v1.0

## System

The Hollow Spire is a system-neutral fantasy exploration campaign.

Internal development uses a modern 2024 fantasy d20 reference framework for encounter pacing, progression, and playtest assumptions. This reference is for development only. Publication-facing manuscripts should not require ownership of any proprietary rules product and should avoid proprietary game, setting, monster, class, spell, and rules-product names.

## Campaign Scale

The Hollow Spire is a **100-floor subterranean world** containing settlements, wilderness, ruins, roads, dungeons, prisons, shrines, and impossible environments.

## Level Progression

- Player characters begin at **level 3**.
- Player characters are expected to reach approximately **level 20 around Floor 89**.
- Floors **90–100** are reserved for epic-level exploration, post-20 advancement, endgame mysteries, and campaign culmination.

## Core Pillars

The Hollow Spire is built around three central pillars:

1. **Exploration**
2. **Contribution**
3. **Legacy**

## Central Theme

Exploration is the central theme of the campaign.

The guiding light of the campaign is:

> Always leave things a little better than you found them.

This may include:

- Repairing lantern outposts.
- Restoring routes.
- Helping explorers.
- Aiding settlements.
- Preserving knowledge.
- Carrying journals home.
- Protecting those in need.
- Creating safer paths for those who follow.

## Campaign Tone

The intended tonal ratio is:

- **67% Dark**
- **33% Hope**

The Spire is dangerous, ancient, and often cruel, but the campaign should never become hopeless. The players are meant to create light in dark places.

## Explorer Creed

> No One Descends Alone.


## Core World Law: The Spire Resists Escape

The Hollow Spire resists flight, teleportation, planar travel, magical fast travel, and route-bypassing effects. Roads must be walked. Routes must be mapped. The campaign assumes that distance, descent, backtracking, supply lines, and restored passages remain meaningful throughout play.

See: `Core_World_Law_The_Spire_Resists_Escape_v1.0.md`.

## Publication Note
