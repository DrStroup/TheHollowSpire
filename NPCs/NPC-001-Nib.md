# NPC-001 — Nib

Status: Draft / Canon NPC

Nib is the first major NPC encountered in The Hollow Spire. He may become companion, scout, friend, or conscience. He is not comic relief or a mascot. He is a survivor.

First Appearance: Book One, Chapter One: The Black Cells. Location: Shattered Corridor.

Description: small even for a goblin; lean; undernourished; large amber eyes; crooked nose; patchwork clothing repaired dozens of times.

Personality: kind, curious, nervous, loyal, talkative, superstitious, resourceful, forgiving.

Canon Rule: Nib is the only goblin on Floor 1.
