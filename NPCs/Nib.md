# Nib

A frightened goblin first encountered trapped beneath rubble in the Black Cells.

## First Impression
Nib is terrified, hungry, suspicious, and completely unremarkable.

## If Rescued
Nib becomes attached to the party and may eventually become one of the most important living symbols of hope in the campaign.

## If Ignored
Nib survives independently, but his future relationship with the party changes.

## If Killed
The campaign continues, but one of its greatest possible long-term payoffs is lost.

## GM Secret
If saved and nurtured by the campaign’s events, Nib may eventually become the Keeper of the final major settlement.
