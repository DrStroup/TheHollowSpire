# Chapter 1: Floor 1 — The Black Cells

## Chapter Overview

**Theme:** Awakening  
**Recommended Sessions:** 1–3  
**Primary Goals:**
- Escape the prison complex.
- Meet Nib.
- Discover the first explorer relics.
- Survive the Breach.
- Reach the Floodwater Passages.

**Major Mysteries Introduced:**
- Why are the prisoners here?
- What caused the Breach?
- Why do the cell numbers seem inconsistent?
- Who left explorer equipment behind?
- Why are lantern symbols carved into places they should not exist?

## Read Aloud: Awakening

> Darkness.
>
> Cold stone presses against your skin.
>
> The air smells of rust, damp earth, and stagnant water.
>
> Somewhere far away, water drips rhythmically.
>
> A faint headache pulses behind your eyes.
>
> You remember your name.
>
> You remember fragments.
>
> Faces. Places. Pieces of a life.
>
> But something important is missing.
>
> You open your eyes.
>
> Iron bars stand before you.
>
> Stone walls surround you.
>
> A number has been carved above your cell door.
>
> Not a name.
>
> Not a title.
>
> A number.
>
> Then, somewhere in the darkness beyond the prison, something groans.
>
> Not a creature.
>
> A structure.
>
> A sound like an entire mountain shifting in its sleep.
