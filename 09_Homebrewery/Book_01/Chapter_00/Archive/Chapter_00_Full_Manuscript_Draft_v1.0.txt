CHAPTER 0 — RUNNING THE HOLLOW SPIRE

0.1 WELCOME TO THE HOLLOW SPIRE

Far beneath the world above lies a vast subterranean frontier known only as the Hollow Spire.

Ancient roads wind through flooded caverns. Lantern-lit settlements cling to the edges of underground seas. Forgotten civilizations sleep beneath layers of stone, waiting for someone brave enough to uncover their stories.

The Hollow Spire is a campaign of exploration, discovery, perseverance, and wonder.

Players take the role of explorers venturing into the unknown, charting dangerous regions, uncovering lost histories, forging relationships with isolated communities, and leaving a legacy for those who follow.

This is not a story about chosen heroes.

It is a story about ordinary people who continue forward when others turn back.

Every expedition leaves a mark.

Every discovery matters.

Every light carried into the darkness helps illuminate the path ahead.

What lies beyond is worth finding.


0.2 THEMES OF THE CAMPAIGN

The Hollow Spire is built around five core themes.

EXPLORATION

The unknown is not an obstacle. It is an invitation.

CONTRIBUTION

The actions of one expedition can change the future for everyone who follows.

LEGACY

Discoveries outlive those who make them.

PERSEVERANCE

The path forward is rarely easy, but it is always worth walking.

TRUTH

Every mystery hides an answer, though finding it may require sacrifice.


0.3 WHAT MAKES THIS CAMPAIGN DIFFERENT

The Hollow Spire is not a traditional dungeon crawl.

While exploration remains central to the campaign, the focus is not on clearing rooms or defeating encounters. Instead, players are encouraged to chart the unknown, uncover forgotten histories, establish relationships with communities, and contribute to a living world that changes as discoveries are made.

Several systems help define the campaign's identity:

THE EXPLORER GUILD

The Explorer Guild serves as both a narrative and mechanical foundation for the campaign. Explorers share discoveries, record maps, undertake contracts, and leave knowledge behind for future expeditions.

DISCOVERY MAPPING

Maps are not static rewards. They are created through exploration. Players are encouraged to record routes, landmarks, dangers, and discoveries as they travel.

CONTRACTS

Settlements and guild chapters provide contracts that encourage exploration and provide meaningful goals beyond combat.

REGIONAL EXPLORATION

The Hollow Spire is organized into regions rather than isolated adventures. Each region contains settlements, wilderness, mysteries, and interconnected stories.

THE MOON LANTERN

As explorers progress deeper into the world, ancient relics begin to reveal paths and secrets that were previously inaccessible. The first of these relics is the Moon Lantern, an artifact whose light reveals what was once hidden.


0.4 RUNNING THE HOLLOW SPIRE

The Hollow Spire rewards curiosity.

Players should rarely feel forced down a single path. Instead, they should be encouraged to ask questions, investigate rumors, explore side routes, and pursue discoveries that interest them.

As the Dungeon Master, focus on presenting opportunities rather than solutions.

Allow discoveries to matter.

Allow maps to matter.

Allow settlements to matter.

Combat remains important, but it should not be the primary measure of success.

The most successful expeditions are often remembered not for the monsters they defeated, but for the truths they uncovered and the paths they opened for those who followed.

Whenever possible, reinforce the campaign's central themes:

- Exploration
- Contribution
- Legacy
- Perseverance
- Truth

Every discovery should feel meaningful.

Every setback should encourage determination rather than punishment.

The world is dangerous, but it should never discourage exploration.


0.5 CREATING EXPLORERS

Characters in The Hollow Spire are explorers.

They may be scholars, scouts, guides, artisans, adventurers, pilgrims, mercenaries, cartographers, or wanderers, but each character should have a reason to venture into the unknown.

Consider the following questions during character creation:

- What discovery do you hope to make?
- What motivates you to keep moving forward?
- What legacy do you hope to leave behind?
- What community or cause do you support?
- What truth are you seeking?

Characters need not be heroes.

Some seek knowledge.

Some seek fortune.

Some seek purpose.

What matters is that they are willing to continue the journey.

The Hollow Spire rewards those who keep going.


0.6 BEGINNING THE JOURNEY

Every expedition begins somewhere.

For the explorers of Book I, that beginning lies within the Black Cells.

Alone, uncertain, and cut off from the world beyond, the characters awaken within an ancient prison whose origins have long since been forgotten.

The answers they seek are not immediately available.

The world will not explain itself.

Instead, the explorers must take the first step.

Beyond the prison walls lies a vast subterranean frontier of settlements, waterways, wilderness, forgotten roads, and ancient mysteries.

The journey begins with escape.

What follows is discovery.

Welcome to the Blackwater Warrens.
