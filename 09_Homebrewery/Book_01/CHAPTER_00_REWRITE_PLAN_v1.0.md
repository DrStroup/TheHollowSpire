# Chapter 0 Rewrite Plan v1.0

## Purpose

Align Chapter 0 with current canon before detailed floor production resumes.

## Required Presentation

The Hollow Spire should be presented as a vast subterranean world, not as one continuous dungeon. The opening prison material belongs to Floor 1 only and should not define the entire setting's player-facing identity.

## Merged Themes

Book 01 foregrounds:

- Exploration
- Contribution
- Legacy
- Perseverance
- Truth

## Hidden Lore Handling

The Warden, judgment, prison truth, and recurrence remain GM-facing mysteries. Chapter 0 may mention strange rumors and contradictory evidence, but should not explain recurrence as a system.

## Primer Replacement Summary

Replace "megadungeon-prison" language with "subterranean world" language. Replace "Cycle of Recurrence" exposition with "Rumors of Return" clues. Preserve Explorer Guild, Lanternkeepers, Chronicle Wall, discovery maps, and No One Descends Alone.
