## Audit Legend

- **Complete:** Ready for publication layout or already locked.
- **Publication Ready:** Content is usable in current manuscript/layout with only minor proofing.
- **Partial:** Usable foundation exists but requires expansion.

- **Outdated:** Superseded by newer canon, scope, terminology, or framework.
- **Missing:** Required publication content is not yet present.
- **Archive Only:** Useful for history or recovery, but not active authority.

- Published material must remain system-neutral.
- Publication text must avoid proprietary game-system and trademarked setting references.
- Guild Marks are removed from canon.
- Discovery Credits and Recovery Caches are deprecated as formal reward categories.
- Rewards should follow the Meaningful Reward Philosophy.
- Book 01 covers Region 001 / Floors 1-12.
- Chapter 0 public title is **The Explorer Guild Primer**.
