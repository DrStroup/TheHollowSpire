# The Hollow Spire — Book 01 Publication Audit

**Version:** v0.01  
**Based on Project State:** v0.04  
**Audit Scope:** Book 01, beginning with Floor 1 publication readiness

## Audit Legend

- **Complete:** Ready for publication layout.
- **Needs Formatting:** Content exists but requires Homebrewery conversion.
- **Needs Canon Review:** Content exists but must be checked against current canon.
- **Needs Mechanics Review:** Requires stat, DC, reward, or encounter balance pass.
- **Needs Art Review:** Requires art reference, asset, or visual audit.
- **Needs Map Review:** Requires viewability, label, scale, or player/DM separation audit.
- **Missing:** Required publication content is not yet present.
