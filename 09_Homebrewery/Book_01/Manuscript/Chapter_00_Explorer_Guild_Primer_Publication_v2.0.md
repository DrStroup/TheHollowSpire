# Chapter 0: The Explorer Guild Primer

## Welcome to the Hollow Spire

The Hollow Spire is a campaign of exploration, discovery, and restoration set within a vast subterranean world known simply as the Spire.

Beneath the surface lie forgotten settlements, abandoned outposts, ancient ruins, and mysteries that have endured for generations. Dangerous creatures roam the depths. Old routes collapse. New paths emerge. Entire communities struggle to survive in places where light is precious and certainty is rare.

Yet the Hollow Spire is not merely a tale of survival. It is a story about what explorers leave behind.

## The Explorer Guild

No one knows exactly when the Explorer Guild was founded. Some claim it began as a loose collection of scouts and cartographers who sought to map the safer passages between settlements. Others believe its origins lie in the earliest expeditions into the deeper reaches of the Spire, when survival depended upon cooperation between strangers.

The Guild charts unexplored territory, records discoveries, investigates reports from the frontier, assists isolated communities, recovers lost knowledge, and maintains the fragile network of routes that allow civilization to endure beneath the earth.

## Discovery

Every explorer enters the Spire searching for something. A discovery is more than simply finding something new. A discovery is something that changes understanding.

## Contracts

Throughout the Spire, settlements face challenges that require capable hands and willing hearts. To meet these needs, communities often issue contracts: formal requests for assistance that may involve delivery, mapping, recovery, investigation, protection, or exploration.

## Travel Within the Spire

The Hollow Spire does not surrender distance easily. Roads must be walked. Supplies must be carried. Lantern posts, bridges, maps, and waystations exist because generations of travelers needed them to survive.

Flight is unreliable within the Spire. Creatures capable of soaring elsewhere find their wings heavy, their lift unstable, and their strength quickly spent. Teleportation and planar travel fare no better. Spells meant to bend distance, pierce planes, or carry travelers elsewhere fizzle beneath the magical pressure of the Spire.

Summoning may still draw something into the Spire, but it does not provide escape. The summoner remains where they stand.

## Exploration & Danger

The Hollow Spire rewards curiosity, but curiosity alone is rarely enough to ensure survival. Successful exploration depends upon preparation as much as courage.

## Horror & Wonder

The Hollow Spire is a place of contradictions: forgotten ruins and thriving settlements, ancient dangers and remarkable beauty. The Spire is not defined solely by danger, nor solely by discovery. It is the tension between the two that makes exploration meaningful.

## Leave the World Better Than You Found It

Exploration alone is not the purpose of the Explorer Guild. The Guild exists because people rely upon one another. Every settlement depends upon routes that must be maintained. Every traveler depends upon those who came before.

Leave the world better than you found it.

## Beginning the Journey

Every explorer begins somewhere. As you venture into the Blackwater Warrens, remember the lessons of the Explorer Guild: seek discovery, respect danger, help those in need, record what you learn, and whenever possible, leave the world better than you found it.
