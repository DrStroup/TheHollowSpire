# Artifact Placement Size Rule v1.0

## Locked Rule

Artifacts must be designed for their final placement size, not for full-size inspection.

Most artifact assets will appear inside Homebrewery at reduced scale. Therefore, each asset must prioritize:

- Strong silhouette
- Clear title or document type
- Immediate visual story
- Large readable focal details
- Minimal reliance on small body text

## Design Guidance

Full-page text density may look impressive during generation but often fails when reduced to sidebar or half-page scale.

For most Chapter 0 artifacts, the reader should be able to identify the artifact's purpose at a glance even if not every line is readable.

## Preferred Ratio

For field-note style assets:

- 50–60% notes, annotations, marks, or document texture
- 20–30% illustration/sketch
- 10–20% stamps, route fragments, equipment details, or marginalia
