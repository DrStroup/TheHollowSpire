# Hollow Spire Transit and Route Integrity Framework v1.0

## Locked Rule
Flight, teleportation, planar travel, and fast travel are generally not allowed to bypass the intended exploration structure of The Hollow Spire.

## Design Purpose
The Hollow Spire is built on route-making, mapping, backtracking, settlement connection, supply management, and physical exploration. Any mechanic or story device that allows characters to ignore distance, elevation, barriers, or route danger must be restricted, transformed, or made exceptional.

## Publication Principle
Explain this as a property of the world, not as a punishment.

Use language such as:

- The Spire resists escape.
- Roads must be walked.
- Wings do not soar here.
- The magical weight of the Spire presses down on shortcuts.

## Flight Standard
Flying creatures may exist, but sustained flight is rare or impossible. Their condition should often be sad, uncanny, or adapted:

- Heavy wings.
- Failed lift.
- Short glides.
- Hop-flight.
- Broken migration instincts.
- Nesting behavior altered by the Spire.

## Teleportation Standard
Teleportation and fast-travel effects fail, fizzle, collapse, misfire harmlessly, or cannot find purchase. They should not provide reliable route bypass.

## Planar Travel Standard
Planar and dimensional escape effects cannot pierce the Spire's metaphysical weight. The Spire is sealed against easy departure.

## Exception Standard
Exceptions must be rare, story-significant, and usually tied to major artifacts, seals, ancient infrastructure, or campaign milestones. An exception should open new exploration, not skip exploration.

## Chapter 0 Requirement
Chapter 0 must introduce this principle through an in-world Guild artifact titled **Travel Within the Spire**. This should appear before Chapter 1 so players and GMs understand that routes, maps, and restoration matter.
