# Guild Certification Framework v1.0

## Locked Distinction

Explorer Guild Crest
= Faction Identity

Explorer Guild Certification Stamp
= Administrative Approval

These are separate assets.

## Certification Meaning

A certification stamp communicates:

- Reward Verified
- Risk Assessed
- Request Reviewed
- Contract Legitimate

## Missing Stamp

A posting without certification may be:

- Unverified
- Rumor
- Fraudulent
- Untested
- High Risk

## Usage

The certification stamp may appear on:

- Contracts
- Notices
- Recruitment Flyers
- Expedition Postings
- Bounties
- Settlement Requests

without implying Guild ownership.
