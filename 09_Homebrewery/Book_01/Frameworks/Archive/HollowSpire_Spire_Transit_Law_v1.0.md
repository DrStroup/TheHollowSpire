# Hollow Spire Spire Transit Law v1.0

## Status
LOCKED CANON FRAMEWORK

## Core Rule
The Hollow Spire resists escape and shortcuts. Exploration, route-finding, mapping, backtracking, and physical travel are foundational to the campaign.

## Flight
Sustained flight is generally not available inside the Spire. Flying creatures can usually manage only short bursts, glides, hops, or assisted movement. Creatures that should soar often experience the Spire as a place of grief: their wings are intact, but the depth itself makes true flight impossible.

## Teleportation and Fast Travel
Teleportation, fast travel, and distance-folding effects generally fail under the magical weight of the Spire. Effects such as dimension door, teleport, teleportation circle, word of recall, plane shift, gate, astral projection, and similar magic fizzle, collapse, or fail to establish a destination.

## Planar Travel
Planar travel cannot pierce the Spire from within except by explicit story exception. The Spire's metaphysical pressure prevents ordinary escape to other planes or distant places.

## Summoning Distinction
Summoning can still bring entities or effects into the Spire, but it does not allow the summoner to leave.

## Design Purpose
- The map matters.
- Roads matter.
- Backtracking matters.
- Route discoveries matter.
- Settlements, waystations, bridges, lantern posts, and supply lines matter.
- Players cannot bypass exploration pillars through flight or teleportation.

## Chapter 0 Presentation
This rule should appear diegetically in Chapter 0 as an Explorer Guild training excerpt, not as a dry rules sidebar. The preferred in-world phrase is:

> Birds do not soar here. Magic cannot carry you away. Roads must be walked.
