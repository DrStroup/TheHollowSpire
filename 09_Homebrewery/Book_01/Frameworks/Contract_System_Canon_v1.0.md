# Contract System Canon v1.0

## Locked Contract Categories

- Guild Certified Contracts
- Public Notices
- Private Requests
- Pilgrim Requests
- Wanted Notices
- Expedition Recruitment
- Emergency Notices
- Settlement Bulletins

## Contract Philosophy

Contracts may include:

- Exploration
- Surveying
- Recovery
- Escort Work
- Deliveries
- Resource Collection
- Route Maintenance
- Hunt Low-Tier Threats

The Explorer Guild values exploration first.

## Reward Guidance

Do not use:

- Guild Marks
- Discovery Credits
- Recovery Caches

Rewards should reinforce:

- Exploration
- Ecology
- Resources
- Relationships
- Discovery
- Training
