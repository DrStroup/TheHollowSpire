# Artifact Authenticity Rule v1.0

## Locked Rule

Artifacts should feel used, handled, damaged, revised, and carried through the Spire.

Avoid assets that look too clean, too freshly printed, or too decorative.

## Preferred Details

Use when appropriate:

- Water stains
- Mud smudges
- Fingerprints
- Fold marks
- Crossed-out notes
- Margin corrections
- Pencil arrows
- Question marks
- Torn corners
- Uneven ink
- Route annotations
- Field sketches

## Purpose

The reader should feel that each artifact came from the world of The Hollow Spire, not from a modern layout template.
