# Hollow Spire Chapter Compression Standard v1.0

## Status
LOCKED for Book 01 publication work.

## Rule
Introductory chapter sections do not require numbered micro-headings such as 0.1, 0.2, 0.3, etc., unless a section is explicitly mechanical, referenced elsewhere, or benefits from technical numbering.

## Chapter 0 Application
Remove numeric labels from Chapter 0 headings.

Use:

- Welcome to the Hollow Spire
- The Explorer Guild
- Discovery
- Contracts
- Exploration & Danger
- Horror & Wonder
- Leave the World Better Than You Found It
- Beginning the Journey

Do not use:

- 0.1 Welcome to the Hollow Spire
- 0.2 The Explorer Guild
- 0.3 Discovery

## Layout Rule
Multiple short sections may share a page when they create cleaner page breaks and stronger composition.

## Goal
Reduce unnecessary page count, create space for art, and avoid making Chapter 0 read like a technical manual.
