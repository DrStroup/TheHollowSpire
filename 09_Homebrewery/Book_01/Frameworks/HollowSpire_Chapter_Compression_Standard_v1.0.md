# Hollow Spire Chapter Compression Standard v1.0

## Locked Rule
Introductory chapter sections should not use technical numbering unless the content is a rule reference, stat block, table, or procedure.

Chapter 0 should remove numeric headings such as:

- 0.1 Welcome to the Hollow Spire
- 0.2 The Explorer Guild
- 0.3 Discovery

and replace them with natural publication headings:

- Welcome to the Hollow Spire
- The Explorer Guild
- Discovery

## Purpose
The chapter should read like a published RPG sourcebook, not a technical outline.

## Layout Impact
Multiple short sections may share a page if they break cleanly.
Sections may continue across pages when needed, provided the break is visually intentional.

## Chapter 0 Target
Compress the current eight content pages into approximately four to five designed pages, preserving the manuscript while creating room for art, documents, and visual anchors.
