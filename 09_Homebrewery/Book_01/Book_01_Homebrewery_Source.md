## How to Use This Book

This book is intended to present the opening arc of *The Hollow Spire* as a usable tabletop adventure module. It includes campaign framing, floor-by-floor adventure content, encounters, NPCs, monsters, maps, discoveries, rewards, and appendix material needed to run the first major section of the Spire.

## Design Pillars

- The Spire is a prison, dungeon, civilization, and judgment engine.
- Exploration matters; teleportation and easy bypasses are restricted.
- Backtracking, discovery maps, and route knowledge are core gameplay.
- The party’s moral choices are tracked through the Warden Ledger.
- The Seven Seals structure provides long-form campaign progression.
- Recurrence, death, rebirth, and judgment shape the mythic arc.

# Campaign Primer

## The Hollow Spire

The Hollow Spire is a vast subterranean megadungeon-prison descending through one hundred floors of buried civilizations, failed experiments, sealed powers, and forgotten judgments.

## The Explorer Guild

The Explorer Guild provides a surface-facing structure for expeditions, recovery work, mapkeeping, rumors, commissions, and survival doctrine.

## Deepborn Civilizations

The lower levels of the Spire are not empty ruins. They contain cultures, survivors, factions, and societies adapted to impossible underground conditions.

## The Seven Seals

Seven major seals, represented by gems or seal-stones, are recovered throughout the larger campaign. Each seal changes access, knowledge, or the party’s relationship to the Spire.

## The Warden Ledger

The Warden Ledger tracks the party’s actions through moral axes such as Mercy, Justice, Ambition, and Sacrifice. These choices should alter outcomes, encounters, and final judgment.

## The Cycle of Recurrence

Some souls in the Spire are cursed to die and be reborn until the Warden judges them worthy. This cycle should be treated as both cosmic punishment and possible path to liberation.

# Running the Hollow Spire

## Tone

Book 01 should begin with confinement, uncertainty, and survival horror, then gradually widen into exploration, mystery, faction discovery, and mythic purpose.

## Core Table Rules

### No Simple Escape

Teleportation, planar travel, and similar bypass effects should not allow easy exit from the Spire unless a specific floor, relic, seal, or story exception allows it.

### Maps Are Partial Truths

Explorer maps, personal journals, pilgrim routes, and investigation maps may contradict each other. Each map should reveal a different usable truth.

### Discovery Is Progress

Locations, routes, hazards, NPC knowledge, relic functions, and faction information should be logged as discoveries.

### Recovery Matters

Rewards are not limited to treasure. Recovered names, relics, routes, lost records, and restored memories are all valid progress.
