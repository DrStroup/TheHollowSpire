Convert Floor 01 into a publication chapter only after the regional spine confirms no upstream structural changes are needed.

- provide complete copy/paste page blocks
- include CSS in the replacement block when needed
- avoid fragments unless explicitly requested

Return to Chapter 00 after the regional spine and first manuscript chapters define what primer content is truly required.

Expected Chapter 00 final additions may include:

- route/rest procedure clarification
- real contract examples from Book 01
- discovery examples from actual floors
- settlement contribution examples
- Moon Lantern foreshadowing without explanation

### Milestone 6 — Full Book Assembly Audit

Audit the complete Homebrewery source, art paths, headers/footers, page numbering, image placement, and export readiness.
