# Chapter 00 Artifact Library Registry v1.0

## Purpose
This registry tracks all diegetic artifact assets planned, generated, placed, revised, or retired for Chapter 00.

## Hard Rule
This registry must be updated every time an artifact asset is planned, generated, renamed, revised, placed, replaced, locked, or retired.

No artifact is considered publication-locked unless this registry has a current entry for it.

---

## Status Legend
- PLANNED: Concept identified; not generated.
- DRAFT: Generated or mocked up; not approved.
- APPROVED: Visual direction approved; may be placed.
- PLACED: Implemented in Homebrewery source.
- LOCKED: Final publication asset.
- REPLACED: Superseded by a newer asset.
- RETIRED: Removed from current publication plan.
