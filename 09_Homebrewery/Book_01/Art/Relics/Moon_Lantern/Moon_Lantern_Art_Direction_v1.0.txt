THE HOLLOW SPIRE
MOON LANTERN ART DIRECTION v1.0

Purpose:
Primary artifact illustration for Book 01.

Narrative Role:
The Moon Lantern is not a weapon. It is a guide. It reveals what was always present but hidden.

Theme:
Truth

Visual Identity:
- Ancient explorer lantern
- Dark silver / blackened metal frame
- Silver-blue moonlight
- Floating pearl or moonlike orb inside the lantern
- Faint revealed glyphs, runes, hidden markings, or unseen paths
- Dark cavern atmosphere

Avoid:
- Firelight
- Sunlight
- Aggressive weapon imagery
- Holy relic presentation
- Overly ornate royal decoration

Approved Composition:
A single suspended Moon Lantern in a dark cavern, glowing with silver-blue light. Hidden symbols and traces of unseen truth appear around it in the darkness.
