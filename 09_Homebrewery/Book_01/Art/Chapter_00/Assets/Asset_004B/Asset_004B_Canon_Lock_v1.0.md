## Player-Facing Text
COMMON TRUTHS OF THE SPIRE

I. Record the journey.
II. Paper doesn’t breathe.
III. Nothing is without consequence.
IV. Remember what we leave behind.
V. Seek ever-farther horizons.
VI. One more step.
VII. A lantern shines brightest in the dark.

## Hidden Design Purpose
These seven truths subtly foreshadow the Seven Virtues / Seven Seals in locked order:
Truth, Mercy, Justice, Sacrifice, Ambition, Perseverance, Hope.

Do not explain the Seven Seals structure in the player-facing artifact.
