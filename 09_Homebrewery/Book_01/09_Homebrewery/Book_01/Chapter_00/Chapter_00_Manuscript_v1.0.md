
# Chapter 0 — Running The Hollow Spire

## 0.1 Welcome to the Hollow Spire

Far beneath the world above lies a vast subterranean frontier known only as the Hollow Spire.

Ancient roads wind through flooded caverns. Lantern-lit settlements cling to the edges of underground seas. Forgotten civilizations sleep beneath layers of stone, waiting for someone brave enough to uncover their stories.

The Hollow Spire is a campaign of exploration, discovery, perseverance, and wonder.

Players take the role of explorers venturing into the unknown, charting dangerous regions, uncovering lost histories, forging relationships with isolated communities, and leaving a legacy for those who follow.

This is not a story about chosen heroes.

It is a story about ordinary people who continue forward when others turn back.

Every expedition leaves a mark.
Every discovery matters.
Every light carried into the darkness helps illuminate the path ahead.

What lies beyond is worth finding.
