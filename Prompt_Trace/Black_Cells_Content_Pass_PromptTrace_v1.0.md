# Black Cells Content Pass v1.0 — Prompt Trace

## Source Context
This package follows the locked Floor 001 Zone Structure v1.1 archival package.

## Major Decisions
- Zone 01 remains small and focused.
- Nib is not introduced in the Black Cells.
- The Codex is not introduced in the Black Cells.
- Lantern Stability is not introduced in the Black Cells.
- The first contradiction is Explorer Map #17.
- The pristine cell concept was rejected because it could halt play while offering no payoff.
- The Explorer's Cell combines mystery, loot, and the first contradiction.
- Iron Rats serve as the first low-threat hostile encounter.
- The Abandoned Guard Post transitions players naturally toward the Rotunda.

## Design Goal
Create a playable opening zone that teaches exploration through environment rather than explanation.

## Protected Canon
Zone 01 must not reveal:
- Recurrence.
- Previous lives.
- Previous descents.
- The true nature of the Spire.
- The identity or full nature of the escaped entity.
