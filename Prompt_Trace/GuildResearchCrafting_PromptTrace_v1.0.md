# Guild Research & Crafting Framework v1.0 — Prompt Trace

## Origin

This framework emerged during Loot Registry review.

The user proposed that loot could have a subpurpose:

Example:
- Party reaches Emberlight.
- Party has 10 Iron Rat Whiskers and other common loot.
- Party has 25 Discovery.
- Guild alchemist trades special potions for materials.

The concern was whether this was gold with extra steps.

## Resolution

The system is acceptable if it avoids generic loot conversion and instead uses:

- Discovery thresholds.
- Specific named materials.
- Guild specialists.
- Recipe-based crafting.
- Contribution rather than selling.

## Key Lock

Loot becomes gold with extra steps if all materials convert into generic value.

Loot becomes Hollow Spire crafting when specific materials unlock specific exploration tools through Guild knowledge.

## Final Direction

Discovery unlocks recipes.

Materials enable recipes.

Specialists craft results.

Crafted rewards should make future exploration more interesting, not merely easier.
