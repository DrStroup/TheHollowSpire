# HANDOUT-001
# Journal of Explorer Edrin Vale

Recovered from a weathered satchel found within the Black Cells.

The pages that remain are water stained and torn. Many entries are missing entirely.

## Day 11
Finally found the route. The old records were right. Mostly. The prison exists, though not in the way I expected. Everything feels wrong here. Too old. Too quiet.

## Day 12
Surveyed the eastern corridors. Found population records. Seventy-six prisoners. Nothing unusual until I found another ledger. Same date. Same signatures. Seventy-seven prisoners. No explanation. Checked both twice. Both appear authentic.

## Day 13
Thought I was losing my mind. Found another contradiction. Cell numbers skip. Others repeat. The walls disagree with the records. The records disagree with each other. No one told me exploration involved accounting.

## Day 14
The lantern symbol appears again. Older than the prison. Impossible. The prison should be the oldest structure on this floor. I am beginning to suspect that assumption is incorrect.

## Day 15
Saw movement. Thought it was another explorer. Was not. Will not be investigating further.

## Day 16
Found an old inscription. Nearly worn away. Most unreadable. One line remains: **The door was never locked.** No idea what that means. Wish I did.

## Day 17
The records say: Floors 1–11. The map says: Floors 1–11. I have counted six times. There are only ten. I feel ridiculous writing this down.

## Day 18
Spoke aloud while checking the records. Could have sworn somebody answered. No one was there. Need proper sleep.

## Day 19
I found the Eleventh.

The remainder of the page has been torn away.

## Final Note
If I do not return, ask Joren. He'll understand the maps. Probably. Hopefully. Actually no. Definitely probably.
